### 1. 职业、实践与专业精神 Profession, Practice and Professionalism

课程区分了三个关键概念：职业、实践和专业精神。The course distinguishes three key concepts: Profession, Practice, and Professionalism.

**职业 Profession：**职业是指需要专门教育、专业知识和实践技能的工作。A profession refers to an occupation that requires specialised education, expert knowledge, and practical skills.

**实践 Practice：**实践是指反复运用知识和技能，以改善工作表现。Practice involves the repeated application of knowledge and skills to improve performance.

**专业精神 Professionalism：**专业精神是指从事专业工作时应具备的行为、态度和职业操守。Professionalism refers to the expected behaviours, attitudes, and conduct within a professional role.

## 第二周：通过 IT 投资创造组织价值 Week 2: Creating Organisational Value from IT Investments

### 1. 组织与组织结构 Organisation and Organisational Structure

#### 1.1 什么是组织 What Is an Organisation?

组织是由一群人按照一定结构共同工作，以实现特定目标的群体。An organisation is a structured group in which people work together to achieve a specific goal or objective.

组织可以是正式的，例如公司、学校、政府机构和非营利组织；也可以是非正式的，例如社区团体或俱乐部。It can be formal, such as a company, school, government agency, or non-profit organisation, or informal, such as a community group or club.

**组织的关键特征 Key Characteristics of an Organisation**

**1. 目标 Purpose：**每个组织都有指导其活动的使命或目标。Each organisation has a mission or goal that guides its activities.

**2. 人员 People：**组织由具有不同角色、责任和技能的人员构成。Organisations involve individuals with different roles, responsibilities, and skills.

**3. 结构 Structure：**组织具有明确的层级，用于说明任务如何分配和协调。There is a clear hierarchy that outlines how tasks are assigned and coordinated.

**4. 系统 System：**组织具有既定的沟通、决策和运营机制。An organisation has established communication, decision-making, and operational systems.

**5. 资源 Resources：**组织会使用人力、财务、技术和实物资源。Organisations utilise human, financial, technical, and physical resources.

#### 1.2 组织和企业有什么区别 What Is the Difference Between an Organisation and a Business?

**组织 Organisation：**组织是一个广泛概念，指一群人以结构化方式共同实现目标。组织的目的不一定是盈利。An organisation is a broad term referring to any group of people working together in a structured way to achieve a common goal. Its purpose is not always profit-driven.

非营利组织的例子包括红十字会；政府组织的例子包括 NSW Health；教育组织的例子包括悉尼大学；其他例子还包括本地体育俱乐部。Examples include non-profits such as the Red Cross, government bodies such as NSW Health, universities such as the University of Sydney, and clubs such as a local sports club.

**企业 Business：**企业是组织的一种，其主要目标是通过提供商品或服务获得利润。A business is a type of organisation whose primary goal is to make a profit by providing goods or services.

企业的例子包括 Canva 等创业公司、Apple 等大型公司，以及本地咖啡店等小型企业。Examples include start-ups such as Canva, corporations such as Apple, and small businesses such as local cafés.

#### 1.3 什么是组织结构 What Is an Organisational Structure?

组织结构定义了任务、责任和权力在组织内部的分配方式。Organisational structure defines how tasks, responsibilities, and power are distributed within an organisation.

它显示组织内部的层级关系、汇报路径，以及不同角色和部门如何互动与协调。It shows hierarchical relationships, reporting routes, and how different roles and departments interact and coordinate.

#### 1.4 为什么理解组织结构很重要 Why Is Organisational Structure Important?

组织结构能够明确谁向谁汇报、界定决策权限、提高效率和沟通质量，并帮助组织管理增长与变化。It clarifies who reports to whom, defines decision-making authority, improves efficiency and communication, and helps manage growth and change.

#### Q1.2 组织结构的类型 Types of Organisational Structures

**1. 职能型组织结构 Functional Organisational Structure**

组织按照特定职能划分为不同部门，例如 IT、人力资源、财务和市场营销部门。An organisation is divided into departments based on specific functions, such as IT, HR, Finance, and Marketing.

**优势 Pros：**专业化分工能够提高效率；角色与责任清晰；组织可以按照部门进行管理和扩张。Specialisation leads to efficiency, roles and responsibilities are clear, and the organisation is easier to manage and scale by department.

**局限 Cons：**部门之间的沟通可能不足，容易形成信息孤岛并降低灵活性。Communication across departments may be limited, creating silos and reducing flexibility.

**适用组织 Best For：**具有明确部门划分的大型或成长型组织，例如科技公司和银行。It is suitable for large or growing organisations with clearly defined departments, such as technology companies and banks.

**有效原因 Why It Works：**各个领域的专业化有助于企业在保持部门专注度的同时进行全球扩张。Specialisation in each area helps companies expand globally while maintaining departmental focus.

**2. 矩阵型组织结构 Matrix Organisational Structure**

员工通常同时向职能经理和项目经理或团队经理汇报。Employees report to more than one manager, typically by function and project or team.

**优势 Pros：**可以在不同项目之间有效使用资源；鼓励团队合作和跨职能技能；提高组织的灵活性与适应能力。It enables efficient use of resources across projects, encourages teamwork and cross-functional skills, and increases flexibility and adaptability.

**局限 Cons：**双重权力关系可能造成混乱，因此需要较强的沟通和协调能力。Dual authority can cause confusion and requires strong communication and coordination.

**适用组织 Best For：**项目型或全球化企业，例如咨询公司、研发组织和 IT 服务公司。It is suitable for project-based or global companies, such as consulting firms, R&D organisations, and IT service companies.

**有效原因 Why It Works：**双重汇报机制既能促进不同项目之间的协作，又能维持技术专业性和创新能力。Dual reporting enables better collaboration across projects while maintaining technical excellence and innovation.

**3. 扁平型组织结构 Flat Organisational Structure**

员工与领导层之间只有很少的中间管理层级，甚至完全没有中层管理人员，因此普通员工更接近决策过程。There are few or no levels of middle management between staff and leadership, so employees are closer to decision-making.

**优势 Pros：**沟通和决策速度较快；鼓励创新与合作；组织更加灵活且适应性更强。Communication and decision-making are fast, innovation and collaboration are encouraged, and the organisation is flexible and adaptive.

**局限 Cons：**角色和责任可能不够清晰，而且这种结构不适合大型团队的扩张。It can create confusion about roles and responsibilities and may not scale effectively for large teams.

**适用组织 Best For：**重视敏捷性和速度的创业公司或小型团队。It is suitable for start-ups or small teams where agility and speed matter more than hierarchy.

**有效原因 Why It Works：**对于游戏等具有创造性和敏捷性的行业，这种结构能够促进创新和快速迭代。For a creative and agile industry such as gaming, this structure fosters innovation and rapid iteration.

**4. 层级型组织结构 Hierarchical Organisational Structure**

层级型结构采用传统的自上而下管理方式，具有多个权力层级和清晰的命令链。It uses a traditional top-down approach with multiple levels of authority and a clear chain of command.

**优势 Pros：**汇报关系和责任明确；结构稳定，适合管理大型团队；支持组织增长和职责委派。It provides clear reporting lines and accountability, is stable and suitable for managing large teams, and supports growth and delegation.

**局限 Cons：**决策速度较慢，并且可能限制员工的创造力和自主权。Decision-making is slower, and the structure can stifle creativity and employee autonomy.

**适用组织 Best For：**政府机构、大型企业和传统公司。It is suitable for government bodies, large enterprises, and traditional corporations.

**有效原因 Why It Works：**对于规模巨大的企业，严格的层级结构能够保证全球运营中的稳定性、控制力和责任落实。In a massive enterprise, a strict hierarchy ensures stability, control, and accountability across global operations.

### 2. IT 在创造组织价值中的作用 Role of IT in Creating Organisational Value

#### 2.1 四个基础概念 Four Foundational Concepts

相关基础概念包括组织、价值、IT 投资和组织价值，以及价值与组织价值之间的关系。The foundational concepts include organisation, value, IT investment, organisational value, and the relationship between value and organisational value.

**价值 Value：**价值是指某个事物的用途、好处或重要程度。Value refers to the worth, usefulness, or benefit of something.

价值可以是有形的，例如金钱和产品；也可以是无形的，例如信任和满意度。It can be tangible, such as money and products, or intangible, such as trust and satisfaction.

价值通常回答的问题是：“这如何使我或其他人受益？”In general, value answers the question: “How does this benefit me or others?”

**组织价值 Organisational Value：**组织价值是组织为利益相关者创造、交付和持续维持的价值。利益相关者包括客户、员工、股东和社会。Organisational value refers to the value created, delivered, and sustained by an organisation for its stakeholders, including customers, employees, shareholders, and society.

组织价值通常回答的问题是：“组织如何为利益相关者创造有意义的结果？”In general, organisational value answers the question: “How does an organisation create meaningful outcomes for its stakeholders?”

#### 2.3 什么是 IT 投资 What Is an IT Investment?

IT 投资是指将财务、人力和技术资源投入信息技术系统、工具或服务，以支持或改善组织的运营、绩效或战略目标。An IT investment refers to the allocation of financial, human, and technological resources to information technology systems, tools, or services to support or improve an organisation’s operations, performance, or strategic goals.

这些资源和能力既可以由组织内部拥有和采购，也可以从外部供应商获得或外包。These resources and capabilities can be owned in-house or procured externally through outsourcing.

**IT 投资示例 Examples：**硬件包括个人设备、服务器和网络；软件包括应用程序、平台和中间件；其他投资还包括数据、人员、流程和管理框架。Hardware includes individual devices, servers, and networks; software includes applications, platforms, and middleware; other investments include data, people, processes, and frameworks.

#### 2.4 资源与能力的区别 Distinction Between Resources and Capabilities

**资源 Resources：**资源是组织拥有的事物。Resources refer to what an organisation owns.

资源可以是有形的，例如房产、机器和硬件；也可以是无形的，例如知识、员工技能和政策。They may be tangible, such as property, machinery, and hardware, or intangible, such as knowledge, employee skills, and policies.

**能力 Capabilities：**能力是组织利用现有资源能够完成的事情。Capabilities refer to what an organisation can do by using those resources.

能力是组织根据现有资源、技能和技术执行特定行动并实现一定结果的能力。A capability is the ability to execute a specified course of action to achieve certain outcomes based on available resources, skills, and technology.

#### 2.5 这些概念之间如何联系 How Do These Concepts Link Together?

**组织 Organisation：**组织由推动运营的结构、人员和流程构成，并为战略制定与价值交付提供基础。An organisation consists of the structure, people, and processes that drive operations and provide the foundation for strategy and delivery.

**IT 投资 IT Investment：**技术能够提高效率、促进创新并支持组织扩张，例如自动化、数据分析、云计算和人工智能。Technology enables efficiency, innovation, and scale through investments such as automation, data analytics, cloud computing, and AI.

**价值 Value：**价值是组织努力与技术赋能共同产生的结果，为客户、员工、投资者和社会等利益相关者带来好处。Value is the result of organisational effort and technological enablement, producing benefits for stakeholders such as customers, employees, investors, and society.

价值成果的例子包括利润、股息和运营效率。Examples include profits, dividends, and operational efficiency.

信息技术在帮助组织创造、交付和持续维持价值方面发挥着核心作用。Information Technology plays a central role in helping organisations create, deliver, and sustain value.

在当今数字化世界中，IT 不只是支持职能，也是促进创新、效率和竞争力的战略推动因素。In today’s digital world, IT is not just a support function; it is a strategic enabler of innovation, efficiency, and competitiveness.

**2. 增强决策能力 Enhance Decision-Making：**使用数据分析、商业智能工具和人工智能支持决策。Use data analytics, business intelligence tools, and artificial intelligence to support decisions.

**3. 推动创新 Drive Innovation：**使用物联网、区块链和智能代理等技术推动创新。Use technologies such as IoT, blockchain, and agentic AI to drive innovation.

#### 1.1 什么是项目 What Is a Project?

项目是为了创造独特的产品、服务或成果而开展的临时性工作。A project is temporary work undertaken to create a unique product, service, or outcome.

**项目的关键特征 Key Features of a Project**

**1. 临时性 Temporary：**项目具有明确的开始日期和结束日期。A project has clear start and end dates.

**2. 独特性 Uniqueness：**即使项目实施过程可以重复，最终产生的成果也不是常规性的。The outcome is not routine, even if the process can be repeated.

**3. 渐进明细 Incremental Elaboration：**项目通常分阶段开展，具体内容会随着项目推进而逐渐增加和完善。A project is developed in stages and progressively refined over time.

**IT 项目的例子 Examples of IT Projects：**开发新的移动应用程序、实施客户关系管理系统，以及迁移至云基础设施。Examples include developing a new mobile application, implementing a CRM system, and migrating to cloud infrastructure.

#### 1.2 项目成功完成的标准 Project Completion Success Criteria

**1. 按时交付 On-Time Delivery：**项目按照约定的时间表完成。The project is completed according to the agreed schedule.

**2. 符合预算 Within Budget：**项目支出保持在分配的成本范围内。The project remains within the allocated costs.

**3. 满足范围和质量要求 Meet Scope and Quality：**交付全部要求的功能，并达到可以接受的质量标准。The project delivers all required features and meets acceptable quality standards.

**4. 利益相关者满意 Stakeholder Satisfaction：**最终用户、客户和管理层对项目成果感到满意。End users, customers, and management are satisfied with the results.

**5. 创造业务价值 Business Value：**项目能够产生可衡量的收益，例如节省成本、提高效率或形成竞争优势。The project delivers measurable benefits such as cost savings, efficiency gains, or competitive advantage.

**1. 项目范围定义不明确 Unclear Scope Definition：**需求不清晰，而且频繁发生变更。Requirements are unclear and change frequently.

**2. 时间表或预算不切实际 Unrealistic Timelines or Budgets：**项目采用了过于乐观的时间或成本估算。Time and cost estimates are overly optimistic.

**3. 利益相关者参与度低 Low Stakeholder Engagement：**项目缺少发起人或最终用户的支持。The project lacks sponsor or end-user buy-in.

**4. 风险管理不足 Inadequate Risk Management：**项目团队未能提前识别、评估和缓解潜在问题。The project team fails to anticipate, assess, and mitigate potential problems.

**5. 沟通不畅 Miscommunication：**不同团队与利益相关者之间的信息和理解不一致。Information and expectations are inconsistent between teams and stakeholders.

**6. 资源问题 Resource Issues：**项目可能缺乏熟练员工，或者受到技术能力限制。The project may lack skilled staff or face technical limitations.

### 2. 项目管理方法论 Project Management Methodologies

项目管理方法论是一套结构化方法，用于指导项目如何进行规划、执行和交付。A project management methodology is a structured approach that guides how projects are planned, executed, and delivered.

项目管理方法论的主要目标是为组织工作提供框架、提高效率和沟通质量、帮助管理风险，并持续交付一致的项目成果。Its main objectives are to provide a framework for organisational work, improve efficiency and communication, help manage risk, and deliver consistent results.

#### Q1.3／Q3.1／Q4.1 瀑布、敏捷与 DevOps Waterfall, Agile and DevOps

#### 2.1 瀑布方法论 Waterfall Methodology

瀑布是一种线性、顺序式方法，每个阶段必须完成后才能开始下一个阶段。Waterfall is a sequential, linear approach in which each stage must be completed before the next stage begins.

**优势 Advantages：**瀑布具有清晰的项目结构和完整的文档；每个阶段都有明确的交付成果，因此比较容易管理；当需求稳定时，这种方法通常能够有效运行。Waterfall provides a clear structure and complete documentation, is easier to manage because each stage has defined deliverables, and works well when requirements are stable.

**局限 Disadvantages：**瀑布缺乏应对变化的灵活性；长期项目的风险较高；项目过度依赖最初编写的文档；在开发过程中，利益相关者的参与程度通常有限。Waterfall lacks flexibility when changes occur, creates greater risk in long-term projects, relies heavily on initial documentation, and provides limited stakeholder involvement during development.

**适用场景 When to Use：**适合项目范围固定、预期变化很少的项目，例如合规软件和建筑项目。It is suitable for projects with a fixed scope and minimal expected changes, such as compliance software and construction projects.

**案例 Example：NASA 航天飞机软件开发 NASA Space Shuttle Software Development**

NASA 选择瀑布方法，是因为项目需求定义明确，而且不太可能发生变化。NASA selected Waterfall because the requirements were well defined and unlikely to change.

最终的软件具有极高的可靠性，但开发过程持续了很多年，而且成本很高。The resulting software achieved extremely high reliability, but development took many years and was costly.

#### 2.2 敏捷方法论 Agile Methodology

敏捷是一种迭代式、灵活的项目管理方法，重点是通过小规模的渐进改进持续交付成果。Agile is an iterative and flexible approach that focuses on delivering small, incremental improvements.

敏捷使用称为“冲刺”的短工作周期。Agile uses short work cycles called sprints.

**优势 Advantages：**敏捷能够适应不断变化的需求，鼓励利益相关者尽早并频繁地提供反馈，而且可以更快地交付可运行的软件。Agile adapts to changing requirements, encourages early and frequent stakeholder feedback, and delivers working software more quickly.

**适用场景 When to Use：**适合需求会不断演变的项目，例如移动应用程序和软件即服务产品。It is suitable for projects with evolving requirements, such as mobile applications and Software as a Service products.

**敏捷宣言 Agile Manifesto**

**1. 个体和互动高于流程和工具 Individuals and Interactions over Processes and Tools：**敏捷更重视团队成员之间的合作，而不只是遵循固定流程和使用工具。Agile values collaboration between people more than simply following fixed processes and using tools.

**2. 可工作的软件高于详尽的文档 Working Software over Comprehensive Documentation：**敏捷重视尽快交付能够实际运行的软件，而不是将主要精力放在编写大量文档上。Agile prioritises delivering functional software rather than focusing mainly on extensive documentation.

**3. 客户合作高于合同谈判 Customer Collaboration over Contract Negotiation：**敏捷强调在开发过程中持续与客户合作，而不是只依靠项目开始时确定的合同内容。Agile emphasises continuous cooperation with customers rather than relying only on the contract agreed at the beginning.

**4. 响应变化高于遵循计划 Responding to Change over Following a Plan：**敏捷允许团队根据新需求和反馈调整项目，而不是机械地执行原有计划。Agile allows the team to adjust the project based on new requirements and feedback instead of rigidly following the original plan.

**案例 Example：Spotify 敏捷小队模式 Spotify’s Agile Squad Model**

Spotify 的敏捷小队模式使团队能够每周发布更新和新功能，有时甚至可以每天发布。Spotify’s Agile squad model enables teams to release updates and new features weekly and sometimes even daily.

#### 2.3 DevOps 方法论 DevOps Methodology

DevOps 将软件开发与 IT 运维结合起来，以实现持续集成、持续交付和持续监控。DevOps combines software development and IT operations to support continuous integration, continuous delivery, and continuous monitoring.

它主要关注自动化、团队协作和快速发布。It focuses on automation, collaboration, and rapid releases.

**优势 Advantages：**DevOps 能够缩短发布周期；通过持续测试和集成提高软件质量；增强开发团队与运维团队之间的协作。DevOps enables faster release cycles, improves software quality through continuous testing and integration, and strengthens collaboration between development and operations teams.

**适用场景 When to Use：**适合云应用程序和高频发布环境。It is suitable for cloud-based applications and high-frequency release environments.

**案例 Example：Amazon 的 DevOps 部署模式 Amazon’s DevOps-Driven Deployment Model**

Amazon 采用由 DevOps 推动的部署模式，平均每 11.7 秒就可以部署一次变更。Amazon uses a DevOps-driven deployment model and deploys a change every 11.7 seconds on average.

#### 4a) 比较 Agile 与 Waterfall 在灵活性和利益相关者参与度方面的差异 Compare Agile and Waterfall in terms of flexibility and stakeholder involvement

**灵活性 Flexibility**

Waterfall 采用线性、顺序式流程。一旦需求确定，后续修改会比较困难，因此灵活性较低。Waterfall follows a linear, sequential process in which changes become difficult once requirements have been established, making it relatively inflexible.

Agile 使用较短的迭代周期，每个冲刺结束后都可以根据反馈调整需求，因此能够快速响应变化，灵活性较高。Agile uses short iterations and allows requirements to be adjusted after each sprint, making it highly responsive and flexible.

**利益相关者参与 Stakeholder Involvement**

在 Waterfall 中，利益相关者主要在项目开始时的需求阶段和项目结束时参与，开发过程中的参与程度有限。In Waterfall, stakeholders participate mainly during the initial requirements stage and at the end of the project, with limited involvement during development.

在 Agile 中，利益相关者会通过冲刺评审、反馈会议和产品演示持续参与每个迭代。In Agile, stakeholders remain involved throughout each iteration through sprint reviews, feedback sessions, and product demonstrations.

#### b) 解释 Agile 如何比 Waterfall 更能降低项目风险 Explain how Agile reduces project risk more effectively than Waterfall

**持续测试和持续反馈 Continuous Testing and Feedback：**Agile 通过频繁测试、增量交付和周期性的客户评审，使问题能够在项目早期被发现并修复。Agile uses frequent testing, incremental delivery, and regular client reviews so that problems can be identified and corrected early.

相比之下，Waterfall 往往在开发后期才对完整系统进行整体测试，因此各种风险可能不断积累，并在项目最后阶段集中暴露。In contrast, Waterfall often tests the complete system near the end of development, allowing risks to accumulate and emerge during the final stages.

因此，Agile 能够减少需求偏差、技术问题、用户不满意以及其他后期失败风险。Agile therefore reduces requirement mismatches, technical problems, user dissatisfaction, and other risks of late-stage failure.

#### c) 说明转向 Agile 时的一个挑战，并提出解决方案 Explain one Agile adoption challenge and provide a solution

**挑战 Challenge：**团队可能难以适应新的敏捷角色和工作方式，例如从固定职责转向自组织团队。Teams may struggle to adapt to new Agile roles and working practices, such as moving from fixed responsibilities to self-organising teams.

**解决方案 Solution：**组织可以提供敏捷培训和角色说明会，并通过持续沟通帮助团队建立敏捷思维和协作方式。The organisation can provide Agile training and role-clarification sessions and use consistent communication to help the team develop an Agile mindset and collaborative working practices.

### 3. 持续 IT 生命周期的兴起 The Rise of the Continuous IT Lifecycle

#### 3.1 业务与 IT 孤岛 Business and IT Silos

在传统开发环境中，开发团队和运维团队通常在相互隔离的环境中工作。In traditional development environments, development and operations teams usually work in separate silos.

**开发团队 Development Team：**主要关注系统功能、产品特性，以及功能性和非功能性需求。The development team focuses on functionality, features, and both functional and non-functional requirements.

**运维团队 Operations Team：**主要关注成本、可靠性、安全性、风险和可管理性。The operations team focuses on cost, reliability, security, risk, and manageability.

这种隔离容易导致目标不一致、沟通不足和交付速度缓慢。This separation can create inconsistent goals, insufficient communication, and slow delivery.

#### 3.2 传统线性方法 Traditional Linear Approach

传统的软件应用生命周期管理通常由一系列线性、顺序式阶段组成。Traditionally, the software application lifecycle has been managed through a linear and sequential set of stages.

瀑布方法是这种项目管理方式中最常见的方法。Waterfall is commonly used for this form of project management.

#### 3.3 从线性模式转向持续模式 From Linear to Continuous Transformation

**传统模式：线性 Legacy Model: Linear**

传统模式是一个单向过程，通常按照“规划、构建、部署、结束”的顺序进行。The traditional model is a one-way process that follows the sequence of planning, building, deployment, and completion.

规划 Plan→构建 Build→部署 Deploy→结束 End

这种模式通常采用规模较大但发布频率较低的版本。It typically relies on large and infrequent releases.

**新模式：持续 New Model: Continuous**

持续模式强调持续进行渐进式改进，并频繁发布规模较小的版本。The continuous model focuses on ongoing incremental improvement and frequent, small releases.

**为什么需要转变 Why Is the Change Necessary?**

快速变化的数字环境要求组织具备更高的敏捷性。同时，客户对快速修复问题和获得新功能的期望也在不断上升。A rapidly evolving digital landscape requires greater organisational agility, while customer expectations for quick fixes and new features continue to increase.

#### 3.4 推动转变的因素 Factors Driving the Shift

**1. 数字化转型 Digital Transformation：**组织需要跟上竞争对手的技术发展速度。Organisations need to keep pace with their competitors.

**2. 客户期望 Customer Expectations：**用户希望快速获得软件更新和问题修复。Users expect rapid updates and fixes.

**3. 云计算与自动化 Cloud and Automation：**云平台和自动化工具能够支持更快速、更安全的部署。Cloud platforms and automation tools enable faster and more secure deployment.

**4. Agile 与 DevOps 实践 Agile and DevOps Practices：**这些实践能够促进团队合作并提高交付速度。These practices promote collaboration and speed.

**5. 数据驱动决策 Data-Driven Decision-Making：**持续监控产生的数据可以帮助组织确定系统改进的优先级。Data obtained through continuous monitoring helps organisations determine improvement priorities.

### 4. 持续 IT 生命周期 Continuous IT Lifecycle

#### 4.1 什么是持续 IT 生命周期 What Is the Continuous IT Lifecycle?

持续 IT 生命周期是一种管理 IT 系统的循环式、迭代式方法，其改进和交付过程不会停止。The continuous IT lifecycle is a cyclical and iterative approach to managing IT systems in which improvement and delivery never stop.

其核心概念是，IT 工作不会在系统部署后结束，而是会继续进行监控、收集反馈和更新。The key concept is that IT work does not end after deployment; monitoring, feedback collection, and updates continue throughout the system’s operation.

这种方式能够使组织更快地进行创新并响应新的需求。This approach enables faster innovation and responsiveness.

**案例 Example：**软件即服务产品会根据用户反馈和系统性能指标持续进行更新。SaaS products are continuously updated based on user feedback and performance metrics.

#### 4.2 持续 IT 生命周期的要素 Elements of the Continuous IT Lifecycle

**1. 规划 Plan：**制定与业务战略保持一致的开发计划。Develop plans that align with the organisation’s business strategy.

**2. 需求、设计、构建与测试 Requirements, Design, Build and Test：**确定需求、设计解决方案、构建或采购系统，并完成测试。Establish requirements, design the solution, build or procure the system, and conduct testing.

**3. 部署与故障排查 Deployment and Troubleshooting：**将系统部署到生产环境，并通过故障排查和维护维持服务水平。Deploy the system to production and maintain service levels through troubleshooting and support.

**4. 监控 Monitoring：**持续监控系统表现，并评估系统为组织创造的价值。Continuously monitor system performance and assess the value it creates for the organisation.

#### a) 概述 IT 系统生命周期的主要阶段及其目的 Outline the main phases of the IT system lifecycle and their purpose

**1. 规划与需求分析 Planning and Requirements：**明确业务需求并定义系统目标。Identify business needs and define system objectives.

**2. 设计与开发 Design and Development：**制定系统架构并构建技术解决方案。Create the system architecture and build the technical solution.

**3. 测试与上线 Testing and Deployment：**验证系统功能、确保可靠性并正式发布系统。Validate functionality, ensure reliability, and release the system.

**4. 运行与维护 Operations and Maintenance：**监控系统表现、应用更新和补丁，并修复问题。Monitor performance, apply updates and patches, and fix issues.

**5. 评估与退役 Review and Retirement：**长期评估系统表现，并在系统过时时规划替换方案。Assess system performance over time and plan its replacement when it becomes outdated.

#### 4.3 线性方法与持续方法的区别 Difference Between Linear and Continuous Methods

| 比较维度 Dimension           | 线性方法 Linear Method                                       | 持续生命周期方法 Continuous Lifecycle Method                 |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **定义 Definition**          | 按照固定步骤依次推进的方法。A step-by-step, sequential approach. | 灵活且不断迭代的方法。A flexible and iterative approach.     |
| **过程 Process**             | 采用单向且结构化的推进过程。A one-way, structured progression. | 采用循环式、迭代式过程。A cyclical and iterative process.    |
| **灵活性 Flexibility**       | 结构僵化，实施变更比较困难。Rigid, making changes difficult to implement. | 适应性较强，可以持续调整。Highly adaptable and open to continuous adjustment. |
| **风险管理 Risk Management** | 风险较高，因为问题通常在后期才被发现。Higher risk because problems are often detected late. | 风险较低，因为系统会接受持续测试和监控。Lower risk because testing and monitoring are continuous. |
| **示例 Examples**            | 传统软件开发和瀑布项目。Traditional software development and Waterfall projects. | 敏捷开发和 DevOps。Agile development and DevOps.             |

#### 4.4 持续 IT 生命周期成功的四项指标 Four Indicators of Continuous IT Lifecycle Success

**1. 发布频率 Release Frequency：**衡量新功能、错误修复或系统更新的部署频率。It measures how often new features, fixes, or updates are deployed.

**2. 平均修复时间 Mean Time to Repair, MTTR：**衡量解决一次安全事件、系统事故或服务中断平均需要多长时间。It measures the average time required to resolve an incident or system outage.

**3. 客户满意度 Customer Satisfaction, CSAT/NPS：**通过客户满意度调查或净推荐值衡量最终用户对系统的满意程度。It measures end-user satisfaction through customer surveys or Net Promoter Scores.

**4. 部署成功率 Deployment Success Rate：**衡量无需回滚且未出现问题的成功部署占全部部署的比例。It is the percentage of deployments that go live without rollbacks or other issues.

#### c) 说明定期生命周期审查如何帮助维持系统性能并控制成本 Recommend how regular lifecycle reviews help maintain system performance and cost control

定期开展生命周期审查可以及时识别系统老化、性能下降和技术过时等问题，使组织能够在问题变得严重之前更新或更换相关组件。Regular lifecycle reviews can identify ageing systems, declining performance, and technological obsolescence, allowing the organisation to update or replace components before problems become severe.

这不仅能够维持系统性能，也可以避免重大故障和紧急维修产生的高额费用，从而降低长期维护成本。This maintains system performance and avoids the high costs associated with major failures and emergency repairs, reducing long-term maintenance expenses.

#### 4.5 持续交付中的常见陷阱 Common Pitfalls in Continuous Delivery

**1. 过度自动化且缺乏充分测试 Over-Automation and Inadequate Testing：**组织可能过度依赖自动化，却没有保留足够的人工检查和质量验证。An organisation may rely too heavily on automation without retaining sufficient human review and quality validation.

**2. 利益相关者沟通不足 Inadequate Stakeholder Communication：**即使团队执行了各种敏捷活动，也可能没有真正与利益相关者进行充分沟通。Stakeholder communication may remain inadequate even when the team follows Agile rituals.

**3. 快速部署中忽视安全 Neglecting Security in Rapid Deployment：**如果团队只关注发布速度，可能会忽略安全测试、漏洞修复和合规要求。If a team focuses only on release speed, it may neglect security testing, vulnerability remediation, and compliance requirements.

### 5. 持续 IT 生命周期与企业架构 Continuous IT Lifecycle and Enterprise Architecture

#### 5.1 什么是企业架构 What Is Enterprise Architecture?

企业架构是一套结构化框架，用于协调组织的业务战略、流程、信息和技术。Enterprise Architecture is a structured framework used to coordinate an organisation’s business strategy, processes, information, and technology.

企业架构的主要目的是确保 IT 投资能够支持业务目标。它既提供当前系统和未来系统的蓝图，也帮助组织弥合战略愿景与实际执行之间的差距。Its primary purpose is to ensure that IT investments support business objectives. It provides blueprints for current and future systems and bridges the gap between strategic vision and execution.

#### 5.2 为什么企业架构很重要 Why Is Enterprise Architecture Important?

**1. 战略对齐 Strategic Alignment：**确保 IT 优先事项与业务优先事项保持一致。It ensures that IT and business priorities are aligned.

**2. 提高效率 Efficiency：**减少系统、工具和工作流程之间的重复与复杂性。It reduces duplication and complexity across systems, tools, and processes.

**3. 提高敏捷性 Agility：**帮助组织快速适应市场、业务需求和技术环境的变化。It enables the organisation to adapt quickly to changes in the market, business requirements, and technology.

**4. 风险管理 Risk Management：**识别系统中的缺口、漏洞以及潜在运营风险。It identifies gaps, vulnerabilities, and potential operational risks within systems.

**5. 成本优化 Cost Optimisation：**避免在功能重叠的工具和重复流程上浪费资源。It prevents resources from being wasted on overlapping tools and redundant processes.

#### Q3.3 企业架构如何支持持续交付 How Does Enterprise Architecture Support Continuous Delivery?

**1. 规划阶段 Planning Phase：**企业架构为技术采用和系统集成制定路线图。EA defines a roadmap for technology adoption and integration.

**2. 设计与构建阶段 Design and Build Phase：**企业架构标准确保新解决方案能够适应组织更广泛的技术和业务生态系统。EA standards ensure that new solutions fit within the broader organisational ecosystem.

**3. 部署与监控阶段 Deployment and Monitoring Phase：**企业架构原则有助于维持不同系统之间的互操作性和整体可扩展性。EA principles maintain interoperability and scalability across systems.

**4. 反馈循环 Feedback Loop：**企业架构会随着新的业务需求和技术变化持续调整。EA evolves in response to new business requirements and technologies.

#### 5.3 企业架构框架 Enterprise Architecture Frameworks

**Zachman 框架 Zachman Framework：**重点关注不同利益相关者看待组织和系统的不同视角。The Zachman Framework focuses on the perspectives of different stakeholders.

**开放组架构框架 The Open Group Architecture Framework, TOGAF：**TOGAF 提供了较为完整的企业架构方法论，并在行业中得到广泛应用。TOGAF provides a comprehensive enterprise architecture methodology and is widely used across industries.

#### Q3.3 企业架构的好处 Benefits of Enterprise Architecture

**1. 战略对齐 Strategic Alignment：**确保 IT 投资直接支持业务目标。It ensures that IT investments directly support business objectives.

**2. 改善决策 Improve Decision-Making：**提供关于系统、流程和数据的整体视图。It provides a holistic view of systems, processes, and data.

**3. 成本优化 Cost Optimisation：**减少系统和服务的重复建设。It reduces duplication across systems and services.

**4. 风险管理 Risk Management：**尽早识别潜在的安全、合规和运营风险。It identifies potential security, compliance, and operational risks early.

**5. 改善协作 Better Collaboration：**打破业务团队与 IT 团队之间的信息孤岛。It breaks down silos between business and IT teams.

**6. 支持持续改进 Continuous Improvement Support：**将企业架构与持续 IT 生命周期结合起来，推动持续创新。It drives continuous innovation in conjunction with the continuous IT lifecycle.

## 第四周：专业沟通、协作与利益相关者管理 Week 4: Professional Communication, Collaboration and Stakeholder Management

### 1. 专业沟通 Professional Communication

沟通是 IT 项目的支柱。有效沟通能够确保开发人员、测试人员、项目经理、客户、用户和外部利益相关者对项目形成一致理解。Communication is the backbone of an IT project. Effective communication ensures consistency among developers, testers, project managers, customers, users, and external stakeholders.

沟通不畅可能造成项目延误、成本超支，甚至导致项目失败。Miscommunication can result in delays, cost overruns, and project failure.

IT 项目本质上具有协作性和跨学科性。沟通对于需求收集、团队协调、利益相关者参与和进度报告都非常重要。IT projects are collaborative and multidisciplinary by nature. Communication is essential for requirements gathering, team coordination, stakeholder engagement, and progress reporting.

项目成功 Project Success=技术 Technology+沟通 Communication

#### 1.1 IT 项目中的沟通类型 Types of Communication in IT Projects

**1. 书面沟通 Written Communication**

正式书面沟通包括电子邮件、报告、合同和项目文档；非正式书面沟通包括 Slack 聊天记录和共享笔记。Formal written communication includes emails, reports, contracts, and project documents, while informal written communication includes Slack conversations and shared notes.

书面沟通应保持简洁清晰，除非确实必要，否则应避免使用专业术语，并在发送前检查语法、拼写和内容。Written communication should be concise and clear, avoid jargon unless necessary, and be proofread before sending.

**2. 口头沟通 Oral Communication**

口头沟通包括面对面交流、线上会议和每日站会。Oral communication includes face-to-face conversations, virtual meetings, and daily stand-up meetings.

口头沟通能够快速澄清问题并建立信任，但参与者需要主动倾听、清楚表达，并合理控制语气和讲话速度。It enables quick clarification and helps build trust, but participants need to listen actively, speak clearly, and manage their tone and pacing.

常见问题是少数人的声音主导整个会议，而其他成员缺少表达机会；如果会议结束后没有书面跟进，重要决定也可能被遗漏。A common pitfall is allowing a few voices to dominate while other members are not heard. Important decisions may also be lost when there is no written follow-up.

**3. 演示沟通 Presentation**

演示可以用于介绍项目提案、提供利益相关者更新和展示项目成果。Presentations are used for project proposals, stakeholder updates, and demonstrations of project outcomes.

有效演示应使用“问题—解决方案—影响”的叙事结构，并合理使用图表、流程图和仪表板等视觉辅助材料。An effective presentation should use a problem–solution–impact narrative and appropriate visual aids such as charts, diagrams, and dashboards.

演示内容还应根据技术受众或非技术受众进行调整。充分练习并控制演示时间能够增强表达者的可信度。Content should also be adapted for technical or non-technical audiences. Practice and effective timing strengthen the presenter’s credibility.

#### Q5.1 为什么沟通对 IT 项目成功至关重要 Why Is Communication Critical to IT Project Success?

沟通是 IT 项目的基础，因为它可以确保所有利益相关者对需求、进度和目标形成一致理解。Communication is fundamental to IT projects because it ensures that all stakeholders share a consistent understanding of requirements, progress, and goals.

良好的沟通可以减少误解和返工、控制范围扩张并提高团队协作效率，从而帮助项目按时、按预算交付并满足用户需求。Effective communication prevents misunderstandings, reduces rework, controls scope creep, and improves coordination, helping the project remain on schedule and within budget while satisfying user requirements.

#### Q5.2 识别两个利益相关者，并说明应如何定制沟通方式 Identify two stakeholders and explain how communication should be tailored to them

**1. 高层管理人员 Executives：**高层管理人员需要高层次且简明的信息，例如项目仪表板、里程碑总结、风险更新和业务价值说明。沟通重点应放在战略影响上，而不是复杂的技术细节。Executives require concise, high-level information such as project dashboards, milestone summaries, risk updates, and business-value statements. Communication should focus on strategic impacts rather than complex technical details.

**2. 最终用户 End Users：**最终用户需要具体、容易理解且具有互动性的沟通，例如产品演示、原型试用和培训课程。沟通重点应放在系统如何影响日常工作、系统是否容易使用，以及如何收集他们的反馈。End users require practical, understandable, and interactive communication such as demonstrations, prototype walkthroughs, and training sessions. The focus should be on usability, effects on daily workflows, and collecting their feedback.

#### Q5.3 推荐一种确保开发期间持续获得反馈的工具或方法 Recommend one tool or method for ensuring continuous feedback during development

项目可以使用敏捷冲刺评审，或者 Microsoft Teams、Slack 等协作平台维持双向沟通。Sprint reviews or collaboration platforms such as Microsoft Teams and Slack can be used to maintain two-way communication.

这些方法允许开发团队定期展示项目进度、快速收集用户意见，并在下一个迭代中立即实施改进，从而形成持续反馈循环。These methods allow developers to present progress regularly, collect user feedback quickly, and incorporate improvements into the next iteration, creating a continuous feedback loop.

#### 1.2 有效沟通框架 Effective Communication Frameworks

**沟通的 7C 标准 The 7Cs of Communication, Q1.4**

**1. 清晰 Clear：**信息应当容易理解，并避免含糊不清的语言。Information should be easy to understand and avoid ambiguous language.

**2. 简明 Concise：**内容应当简短并直接说明重点，避免加入不必要的细节。The message should be brief and focused, avoiding unnecessary details.

**3. 具体 Concrete：**应使用具体事实、数据或例子，而不是抽象陈述。Concrete facts, data, or examples should be used instead of abstract statements.

**4. 正确 Correct：**应确保信息准确，并且不存在语法、拼写或事实错误。The information should be accurate and free from grammatical, spelling, and factual errors.

**5. 连贯 Coherent：**信息应符合逻辑、结构清楚且自然衔接。The message should be logical, well structured, and flow naturally.

**6. 完整 Complete：**应提供接收者采取行动所需的全部信息，避免因内容缺失而产生后续问题。All information required by the recipient should be provided so that missing details do not create further problems.

**7. 礼貌 Courteous：**沟通应保持尊重、积极和礼貌，从而鼓励合作。Communication should remain respectful, positive, and polite to encourage collaboration.

**SBAR 沟通框架 SBAR Communication Framework**

**1. 情况 Situation：**清楚、简短地说明当前发生的问题。Clearly and briefly state the current problem.

**2. 背景 Background：**提供与问题相关的背景和历史信息。Provide relevant background and history.

**3. 评估 Assessment：**说明自己对问题的分析、判断和发现。Present an analysis, judgement, or findings concerning the issue.

**4. 建议 Recommendation：**提出接下来应采取的行动或解决方案。Recommend the next action or proposed solution.

SBAR 可以概括为：目前的情况是什么、之前发生了什么、问题为什么发生，以及接下来应该采取什么计划。SBAR explains what is happening now, what happened previously, why the problem occurred, and what should be done next.

#### 1.3 IT 团队中的沟通障碍 Communication Barriers in IT Teams

**1. 技术术语和缩略语 Technical Terms and Acronyms：**过度使用专业术语可能使非技术人员无法理解信息。Excessive jargon may prevent non-technical stakeholders from understanding the message.

**2. 文化和语言差异 Cultural and Linguistic Differences：**不同语言习惯和文化背景可能造成误解。Different linguistic conventions and cultural backgrounds can create misunderstandings.

**3. 远程协作问题 Remote Collaboration Challenges：**时区差异和线上沟通限制可能降低协作效率。Time-zone differences and limitations of virtual communication can reduce collaboration efficiency.

**4. 主观假设和缺少主动倾听 Assumptions and Lack of Active Listening：**成员可能根据自己的理解作出假设，却没有真正确认对方的意思。Team members may make assumptions based on their own understanding without confirming what others mean.

**5. 文档质量不足 Poor Documentation：**文档不完整或没有及时更新会形成知识缺口。Incomplete or outdated documentation creates knowledge gaps.

### 2. 团队合作与跨文化管理 Teamwork and Cross-Cultural Management

#### 2.1 团队合作的重要性 Importance of Teamwork

IT 项目本质上需要协作，通常需要开发人员、测试人员、设计人员、项目经理和业务分析师共同贡献不同的专业能力。IT projects are collaborative by nature and combine the expertise of developers, testers, designers, project managers, and business analysts.

良好的团队合作能够加快问题解决、提高创造力和创新能力、形成共同责任与问责，并改善项目成果。Effective teamwork leads to faster problem-solving, greater creativity and innovation, shared responsibility and accountability, and stronger project outcomes.

#### 2.2 关于团队合作的错误观念 False Beliefs about Teamwork

**1. “团队合作意味着所有人都必须同意” “Teamwork Means Everyone Must Agree”：**这种理解并不正确。健康的意见分歧可以产生更好的决策和成果。This is incorrect because healthy disagreements can produce better decisions and outcomes.

**2. “团队合作会拖慢工作速度” “Teamwork Slows Work Down”：**有效协作实际上能够减少返工和错误，从而节省时间。Effective collaboration actually reduces rework and errors, saving time.

**3. “优秀团队不会发生冲突” “Good Teams Do Not Have Conflicts”：**冲突是正常现象，关键在于团队如何解决冲突。Conflict is normal; what matters is how the team resolves it.

**4. “团队合作意味着所有人的贡献完全相同” “Teamwork Means Equal Contribution from Everyone”：**不同角色会以不同方式作出贡献，因此贡献不一定具有相同形式。Different roles contribute in different ways, so contributions do not need to take identical forms.

#### 2.3 高效 IT 团队 Effective IT Teams

高效团队通常具有清晰的角色和责任、成员之间的信任和心理安全、开放且尊重的沟通、共同的目标和责任，以及适应变化的能力。Effective teams usually have clear roles and responsibilities, trust and psychological safety, open and respectful communication, shared goals and accountability, and the ability to adapt to change.

团队还需要在技术技能与软技能之间保持平衡。A team also needs an appropriate balance between technical and soft skills.

#### 2.4 团队合作与多样性 Teamwork and Diversity

多元化团队通常能够形成更广阔的视角，并产生更多创新。Diverse teams usually provide broader perspectives and greater innovation.

多样性可以体现在文化和语言、教育和技术背景、性别、年龄以及工作经验等方面。Diversity may involve culture and language, educational and technical background, gender, age, and professional experience.

多样化团队能够改善问题解决能力并开发更加以用户为中心的产品，但也可能遇到沟通障碍和无意识偏见。Diverse teams can improve problem-solving and produce more user-centred products, but they may also face communication barriers and unconscious bias.

#### 2.5 团队动态与挑战 Team Dynamics and Challenges

常见挑战包括成员参与程度不平等、强势成员与安静贡献者之间产生冲突、团队目标不一致或期望不清晰，以及跨文化误解。Common challenges include unequal participation, conflict between dominant members and quieter contributors, inconsistent goals or unclear expectations, and cross-cultural misunderstandings.

远程团队还可能受到时区差异、成员参与度不足和线上互动受限等问题影响。Remote teams may also be affected by time-zone differences, low engagement, and limitations in virtual interaction.

#### 2.6 团队发展模型：塔克曼阶段 Team Development Model: Tuckman Stages, 6.1

**1. 形成期 Forming：**成员开始认识彼此，并了解团队目标和基本职责。Members become acquainted and begin to understand the team’s goals and basic responsibilities.

**2. 震荡期 Storming：**成员开始表达不同意见，可能围绕任务、权力和工作方式发生冲突。Members express different opinions, and conflict may arise over tasks, authority, and working methods.

**3. 规范期 Norming：**成员形成共同规范，并开始以团队成员的身份相互合作。Members develop shared norms and begin collaborating as part of the team.

**4. 执行期 Performing：**团队能够高效合作，并共同推进任务和实现目标。The team collaborates effectively and makes progress towards its objectives.

**5. 解散期 Adjourning：**团队完成任务并取得成果，随后结束或重新分配成员。The team completes its work, achieves its outcomes, and then disbands or reallocates its members.

#### 2.7 贝尔宾团队角色 Belbin’s Team Roles, 6.2

**社交型角色 Social Roles：**包括资源调查者、团队工作者和协调者。These include the Resource Investigator, Team Worker, and Coordinator.

**思考型角色 Thinking Roles：**包括创新者、监督评估者和专家。These include the Plant, Monitor Evaluator, and Specialist.

**行动或任务型角色 Action or Task Roles：**包括塑造者、执行者和完成者。These include the Shaper, Implementer, and Completer Finisher.

#### 2.8 心理安全：Google 亚里士多德项目 Psychological Safety: Google’s Project Aristotle

心理安全是指团队成员共同相信，自己可以在不必担心受到羞辱或惩罚的情况下承担人际风险。Psychological safety is a shared belief that team members can take interpersonal risks without fear of embarrassment or punishment.

Google 亚里士多德项目发现，心理安全是影响团队成功的首要因素。Google’s Project Aristotle found that psychological safety was the most important factor in team success.

在 IT 项目中，心理安全能够促进创新和创造力，减少成员说错话的恐惧，并提高参与度、士气以及压力环境下的韧性。In IT projects, psychological safety promotes innovation and creativity, reduces the fear of saying the wrong thing, and improves engagement, morale, and resilience under pressure.

**如何建立心理安全 How to Build Psychological Safety：**管理者应鼓励成员提出问题和表达不同意见，以尊重的方式回应错误，并将错误视为学习机会。Leaders should encourage questions and different opinions, respond to mistakes respectfully, and treat errors as learning opportunities.

团队还应为所有成员提供平等的表达机会，避免公开指责个人，并对成员提出的反馈采取实际行动。The team should provide equal opportunities for members to speak, avoid publicly blaming individuals, and take meaningful action in response to feedback.

#### 2.9 敏捷团队合作实践 Agile Teamwork Practices

**1. 每日站会 Daily Stand-Up：**通常不超过 15 分钟，用于快速同步工作进度、下一步任务和当前障碍。It usually lasts no longer than 15 minutes and is used to synchronise progress, next steps, and current blockers.

**2. 冲刺规划 Sprint Planning：**团队共同决定本次冲刺需要完成的工作。The team decides what work should be completed during the sprint.

**3. 冲刺评审 Sprint Review：**团队展示和评估已经完成的成果，并从利益相关者处获得反馈。The team demonstrates and evaluates completed work and obtains stakeholder feedback.

**4. 冲刺回顾 Sprint Retrospective：**团队讨论哪些做法有效，以及哪些方面需要改进。The team discusses what worked well and what could be improved.

#### 2.10 工作场所冲突 Workplace Conflict, 7.1

工作场所冲突的常见来源包括竞争性优先事项、沟通不畅、个性冲突、职责不明确和资源限制。Common sources of workplace conflict include competing priorities, miscommunication, personality clashes, ambiguous responsibilities, and resource constraints.

**托马斯—基尔曼冲突模式 Thomas–Kilmann Conflict Mode Instrument, TKI：**该模型基于两个维度，即自信性和合作性。The model is based on two dimensions: assertiveness and cooperativeness.

自信性表示个人对自身目标和利益的关注程度，合作性表示个人对他人目标和利益的关注程度。Assertiveness reflects concern for one’s own goals and interests, while cooperativeness reflects concern for the goals and interests of others.

**任务冲突 Task Conflict：**也称认知冲突，指成员对工作问题存在不同意见。适当的任务冲突可以促进思考并产生建设性结果。Also called cognitive conflict, it involves disagreement about work-related issues. An appropriate level of task conflict can encourage reflection and produce constructive outcomes.

**关系冲突 Relationship Conflict：**也称情绪冲突，通常源于成员之间的个人不相容，并具有较强破坏性。Also called emotional conflict, it is based on personal incompatibility and is often destructive.

### 3. 利益相关者管理与谈判 Stakeholder Management and Negotiation

#### 3.1 谁是利益相关者 Who Are the Stakeholders?

利益相关者是受到项目影响、能够影响项目，或者对项目具有兴趣的个人、群体或组织。Stakeholders are individuals, groups, or organisations that are affected by, can influence, or have an interest in a project.

IT 项目中的利益相关者包括客户、最终用户、项目发起人、供应商、开发人员、测试人员、监管机构和高层管理人员。Stakeholders in IT projects include customers, end users, project sponsors, vendors, developers, testers, regulators, and executives.

#### 3.2 为什么利益相关者管理很重要 Why Is Stakeholder Management Important?

利益相关者管理可以使项目交付成果与各方期望保持一致、减少冲突和范围扩张，并建立信任与项目可信度。Stakeholder management aligns expectations with project deliverables, minimises conflict and scope creep, and builds trust and project credibility.

#### Q4.3 利益相关者分析与映射 Stakeholder Analysis and Mapping

**1. 识别利益相关者 Identify Stakeholders：**识别内部和外部利益相关者。Identify both internal and external stakeholders.

**2. 评估利益和影响 Assess Interests and Influence：**了解他们的利益、期望和影响能力。Assess their interests, expectations, and ability to influence the project.

**3. 确定优先级 Prioritise Stakeholders：**根据利益相关者的重要程度确定管理优先级。Prioritise stakeholders according to their importance.

**4. 制定参与策略 Develop Engagement Strategies：**针对不同利益相关者制定相应的沟通和参与方法。Develop tailored communication and engagement approaches for different stakeholders.

#### Q4.3 权力—利益矩阵 Power–Interest Matrix

权力—利益矩阵根据利益相关者的权力和兴趣程度进行分类。The Power–Interest Matrix categorises stakeholders according to their power and level of interest.

**权力 Power：**利益相关者影响项目决定和结果的能力。The stakeholder’s ability to influence project decisions and outcomes.

**利益 Interest：**利益相关者对项目的关注程度或参与程度。The stakeholder’s level of attention to or engagement with the project.

该矩阵可以帮助项目团队决定，应当投入多少时间和精力管理不同利益相关者。The matrix helps the project team decide how much time and effort should be invested in managing each stakeholder.

#### Q6 a) 使用权力—利益矩阵对三个利益相关者分级，并说明管理方式 Use the Power–Interest Matrix to classify three stakeholders and explain how they should be managed

**1. IT 部门：高权力、高兴趣——密切管理 IT Department: High Power, High Interest—Manage Closely**

IT 部门是系统实施的核心，并且高度关注技术结果，因此需要持续参与决策、参加频繁会议并进行原型评审和演示。The IT department drives implementation and cares strongly about technical outcomes, so it requires continuous involvement in decisions, frequent meetings, prototype reviews, and demonstrations.

**2. 财务部门：高权力、中等兴趣——保持满意 Finance Department: High Power, Medium Interest—Keep Satisfied**

财务部门能够影响项目预算和合规决定，但不需要参与所有技术细节。项目团队应向其提供关键里程碑、预算状况和风险摘要。Finance influences budgeting and compliance decisions but does not need to participate in every technical detail. It should receive updates on key milestones, budget status, and major risks.

**3. 人力资源部门：低权力、高兴趣——保持知会 HR Department: Low Power, High Interest—Keep Informed**

人力资源部门会受到 ERP 系统变化的直接影响，但通常缺少较高的项目决策权。项目团队应提供定期更新、系统试用和培训机会，并建立反馈渠道。HR is strongly affected by ERP changes but generally has limited decision-making authority. It should receive regular updates, system trials, training opportunities, and channels for feedback.

#### Q6 b) SBAR 如何帮助向管理层结构化地沟通项目进度 How Does SBAR Help Structure Project Updates for Management?

**1. 情况 Situation：**说明目前正在发生什么，例如部门之间协作不佳。Explain what is happening now, such as ineffective collaboration between departments.

**2. 背景 Background：**概述问题发生的相关背景，例如各部门的期望不一致。Provide the relevant context, such as inconsistent expectations across departments.

**3. 评估 Assessment：**分析问题及其可能产生的影响，例如上线风险、员工困惑和项目延误。Analyse the issue and its likely effects, such as deployment risks, employee confusion, and project delays.

**4. 建议 Recommendation：**提出可执行的解决方案，例如统一沟通节奏或建立跨部门例会。Recommend practical actions, such as establishing a consistent communication schedule or cross-department meetings.

SBAR 提供了一种简洁、基于事实且重点清晰的结构，使高级管理人员能够快速了解情况并作出更充分的决策。SBAR provides a concise, evidence-based, and focused structure that helps senior management understand the situation quickly and make better-informed decisions.

#### Q6 c) 推荐一种增强跨部门协作的数字工具或方法 Recommend one digital tool or method for improving cross-department collaboration

组织可以使用 Microsoft Teams 频道或共享的 Asana、Jira 看板，使 IT、财务和人力资源部门能够在同一个平台查看任务、讨论问题并共享文件。The organisation can use Microsoft Teams channels or a shared Asana or Jira board so that IT, Finance, and HR can view tasks, discuss issues, and share files on the same platform.

这种方法可以提高项目透明度，支持共享任务追踪和实时互动，从而改善跨部门参与和协作。This improves project transparency, enables shared task tracking and real-time interaction, and strengthens cross-department engagement and collaboration.

#### 3.3 显著性模型 Salience Model, 7.4

显著性模型根据三个属性对利益相关者进行分类。The Salience Model classifies stakeholders according to three attributes.

**1. 权力 Power：**利益相关者影响项目的能力。The stakeholder’s ability to influence the project.

**2. 合法性 Legitimacy：**利益相关者参与项目是否具有适当性和正当性。The appropriateness and validity of the stakeholder’s involvement.

**3. 紧迫性 Urgency：**利益相关者的要求需要多快得到关注。How quickly the stakeholder’s claims require attention.

**确定型利益相关者 Definitive Stakeholders：**同时拥有权力、合法性和紧迫性，应获得最高优先级。They possess power, legitimacy, and urgency and should receive the highest priority.

**支配型利益相关者 Dominant Stakeholders：**同时拥有权力和合法性，具有较强影响力，需要主动管理。They possess power and legitimacy, have strong influence, and require active management.

**依赖型利益相关者 Dependent Stakeholders：**同时具有合法性和紧迫性，但需要依靠其他有权力的利益相关者提供支持。They possess legitimacy and urgency but depend on more powerful stakeholders for support.

**危险型利益相关者 Dangerous Stakeholders：**同时具有权力和紧迫性，如果遭到忽视，可能对项目造成严重影响。They possess power and urgency and may cause serious disruption if ignored.

**休眠型利益相关者 Dormant Stakeholders：**只拥有权力，目前参与程度较低，但在被动员后可能产生影响。They possess power only and currently have limited involvement but may become influential when mobilised.

**酌情型利益相关者 Discretionary Stakeholders：**只具有合法性，参与度通常较低，但对组织声誉可能具有支持作用。They possess legitimacy only, generally require limited engagement, but may support organisational reputation.

**苛求型利益相关者 Demanding Stakeholders：**只具有紧迫性，容易引起注意，但实际影响力相对有限。They possess urgency only and may attract attention, but their actual influence is limited.

#### 3.4 利益相关者参与策略 Stakeholder Engagement Strategies

参与策略必须与利益相关者所属类别相匹配。Engagement strategies must be matched to the relevant stakeholder category.

**1. 通知 Inform：**对于权力和利益都较低的利益相关者，可以通过新闻简报和电子邮件传递信息。For stakeholders with low power and low interest, information can be provided through newsletters and emails.

**2. 咨询 Consult：**主动询问受到项目影响者的意见。Seek the views of people affected by the project.

**3. 参与 Involve：**邀请利益相关者参加研讨会和反馈会议。Invite stakeholders to participate in workshops and feedback sessions.

**4. 协作 Collaborate：**与利益相关者共同创建解决方案并参与决策。Co-create solutions and make decisions together with stakeholders.

**5. 授权 Delegate：**向适当的利益相关者授予决策权，例如向敏捷项目中的产品负责人授权。Give decision-making authority to an appropriate stakeholder, such as a Product Owner in an Agile project.

#### 3.5 利益相关者管理的挑战 Challenges in Stakeholder Management

**1. 需求持续变化 Changing Requirements：**利益相关者的要求可能在整个项目生命周期中不断变化。Stakeholder requirements may change throughout the project lifecycle.

**2. 利益冲突 Conflicts of Interest：**不同利益相关者群体可能具有相互冲突的目标。Different stakeholder groups may have conflicting goals.

**3. 文化和语言障碍 Cultural and Language Barriers：**全球 IT 团队可能因文化和语言差异产生误解。Global IT teams may experience misunderstandings due to cultural and linguistic differences.

**4. 不切实际的期望 Unrealistic Expectations：**利益相关者可能对项目范围、成本、时间或成果抱有不切实际的期望。Stakeholders may have unrealistic expectations concerning project scope, cost, schedule, or outcomes.

**5. 未能尽早识别隐藏的利益相关者 Hidden Stakeholders：**如果没有尽早发现隐藏的利益相关者，项目后期可能出现新的要求和阻力。Failure to identify hidden stakeholders early may introduce new requirements and resistance later.

**6. 沟通过多或不足 Excessive or Insufficient Communication：**对某些群体沟通过多可能浪费时间，而沟通不足又可能导致误解和失去信任。Over-communicating with some groups may waste time, while insufficient communication may cause misunderstandings and loss of trust.

#### 3.6 利益相关者管理的最佳实践 Best Practices for Stakeholder Management

**1. 尽早开展分析 Early Analysis：**在项目早期开展利益相关者分析，并定期更新分析结果。Begin stakeholder analysis early and update it regularly.

**2. 使用清晰的沟通框架 Clear Communication Frameworks：**使用 7C 和 SBAR 等框架提高沟通质量。Use frameworks such as the 7Cs and SBAR to improve communication quality.

**3. 管理期望 Manage Expectations：**避免承诺不足却交付过多，或者作出无法实现的承诺，应使项目承诺与实际交付能力保持一致。Avoid under-managing expectations, excessive delivery commitments, or promises that cannot be fulfilled; align commitments with realistic delivery capacity.

**4. 建立信任和透明度 Build Trust and Transparency：**主动披露重要进展、风险和限制。Communicate important progress, risks, and limitations openly.

**5. 记录决定与协议 Document Decisions and Agreements：**保存重要决策、责任分配和利益相关者协议的书面记录。Maintain written records of important decisions, responsibilities, and stakeholder agreements.

**6. 平衡沟通时间 Balance Engagement Time：**根据利益相关者的重要性合理分配沟通时间和精力。Allocate communication time and effort according to stakeholder importance.

**7. 建立持续参与机制 Maintain Regular Engagement：**通过评审、反馈循环和产品演示维持利益相关者参与。Maintain stakeholder engagement through reviews, feedback loops, and product demonstrations.

## 第五周：循证实践与 IT 项目估算 Week 5: Evidence-Based Practice and Estimation of IT Projects

### 1. 信息与循证实践 Information and Evidence-Based Practice

#### 1.1 什么是信息 What Is Information?

信息是经过收集、处理、组织和结构化的数据，能够以有意义的方式传达知识、观点或指令。Information is data that has been collected, processed, organised, and structured meaningfully to communicate knowledge, ideas, or instructions.

#### 1.2 为什么信息在 IT 专业实践中很重要 Why Is Information Important in IT Professional Practice?

**1. 决策 Decision-Making：**准确的信息能够帮助 IT 专业人员比较不同选择并作出合理判断。Accurate information helps IT professionals compare alternatives and make sound decisions.

**2. 利益相关者沟通 Stakeholder Communication：**清晰的信息能够帮助不同利益相关者形成一致理解。Clear information helps stakeholders develop a shared understanding.

**3. 问题解决与故障排查 Problem-Solving and Troubleshooting：**系统日志、错误记录和性能数据可以帮助技术人员识别问题原因。System logs, error records, and performance data help technical staff identify the causes of problems.

**4. 项目管理 Project Management：**项目经理需要使用进度、成本、范围和风险信息规划并控制项目。Project managers use schedule, cost, scope, and risk information to plan and control projects.

**5. 知识共享与持续学习 Knowledge Sharing and Continuous Learning：**可靠的信息可以被整理为组织知识，并支持持续改进。Reliable information can be transformed into organisational knowledge and support continual improvement.

**6. 合规与伦理 Compliance and Ethics：**IT 专业人员必须根据法律、行业规范和职业准则正确处理信息。IT professionals must handle information appropriately according to laws, industry standards, and professional codes.

#### 1.3 数据—信息—知识—智慧层级 DIKW Hierarchy

DIKW 层级由数据、信息、知识和智慧四个层次组成。The DIKW hierarchy consists of four levels: Data, Information, Knowledge, and Wisdom.

**1. 数据 Data：**没有上下文的原始事实、数字或符号。Raw facts, numbers, or symbols without context.

**2. 信息 Information：**经过有意义处理或组织的数据。Data that has been meaningfully processed or organised.

**3. 知识 Knowledge：**与经验、上下文和解释相结合的信息。Information combined with experience, context, and interpretation.

**4. 智慧 Wisdom：**利用知识作出正确判断和合理决策的能力。The ability to use knowledge to make sound judgements and decisions.

#### 1.4 不同 IT 角色需要什么层次的信息或知识 What Level of Information or Knowledge Is Required?

**数据层 Data Level：**程序员、系统管理员和分析师等技术专家需要处理原始日志、记录和指标。Technical specialists such as programmers, system administrators, and analysts need to process raw logs, records, and metrics.

**信息层 Information Level：**所有 IT 专业人员都需要信息来进行决策、沟通和报告。这是 IT 专业实践所要求的最低层次。All IT professionals need information for decision-making, communication, and reporting. This is the minimum level expected in professional IT practice.

**知识层 Knowledge Level：**项目经理、咨询顾问、业务分析师和高级工程师需要解释并应用信息。Project managers, consultants, business analysts, and senior engineers need to interpret and apply information.

**智慧层 Wisdom Level：**领导者、战略人员、架构师和高管需要作出长期、合乎伦理并具有战略性的决策。Leaders, strategists, architects, and executives need wisdom to make long-term, ethical, and strategic decisions.

#### 1.5 为什么可信信息很重要 Why Is Trustworthy Information Important?

**1. 提高决策准确性 Accuracy in Decision-Making：**决策质量取决于支持决策的信息质量，不准确或具有误导性的信息可能造成高昂损失。Decisions are only as good as the information behind them, and inaccurate or misleading information can lead to costly mistakes.

**2. 建立信任和专业声誉 Trust and Professional Reputation：**客户、管理人员和用户依赖 IT 专业人员提供的信息。分享可信且基于证据的信息能够建立信任。Clients, managers, and users rely on IT professionals. Sharing trustworthy, evidence-based information builds trust.

**3. 降低风险 Risk Reduction：**可靠信息能够帮助组织正确识别风险并避免错误选择。Reliable information helps organisations identify risks correctly and avoid poor choices.

**4. 支持合规与伦理 Compliance and Ethics：**许多行业要求 IT 专业人员使用经过验证且准确的信息；使用不可信信息可能造成违法行为。Many industries require IT professionals to handle verified and accurate information; using untrustworthy information may result in legal violations.

**5. 提高沟通效率 Efficient Communication：**团队和利益相关者需要一个共同、可靠的事实来源。Teams and stakeholders need a common and reliable source of truth.

#### 1.6 信息来源 Sources of Information

**1. 一手来源 Primary Sources：**直接、原始或第一手证据，例如研究报告、调查、访谈、公司记录和系统日志。These are direct, original, or first-hand forms of evidence, such as research reports, surveys, interviews, company records, and system logs.

**2. 二手来源 Secondary Sources：**对一手数据的解释、总结或分析，例如教科书、技术文档、行业报告和新闻文章。These interpret, summarise, or analyse primary data, including textbooks, technical documentation, industry reports, and news articles.

**3. 三手来源 Tertiary Sources：**汇集一手和二手来源形成的资料，例如百科全书、词典和数据库。These are compiled collections of primary and secondary sources, such as encyclopaedias, dictionaries, and databases.

#### 1.7 有用信息与无用信息 Useful and Useless Information

**有用信息 Useful Information：**应当准确、相关、及时、完整、可信、容易理解并且可以支持行动。Useful information is accurate, relevant, timely, complete, credible, understandable, and actionable.

例如：“上个月服务器正常运行时间为 99.95%。”For example: “Server uptime was 99.95% last month.”

**无用信息 Useless Information：**可能不准确、不相关、过时、不完整、重复、无法验证或表述含糊。Useless information may be inaccurate, irrelevant, outdated, incomplete, redundant, unverifiable, or vague.

例如：“去年某个时候，一些服务器可能出现过故障。”For example: “Some servers may have gone down sometime last year.”

#### 1.8 信息有效性的三个层次 Three Levels of Information Validity

信息有效性需要考虑精确性、可信度以及是否正确反映现实。Information validity involves precision, credibility, and correct conformity with reality.

**1. 高有效性 High Validity：**如果信息错误会产生严重后果，就必须保证高度准确，例如医疗记录。High validity is required when errors may cause serious consequences, such as in medical records.

**2. 中等有效性 Medium Validity：**日常 IT 运营需要这类信息。虽然可能允许少量误差，但应将误差降到最低。Medium validity is required for daily IT operations, where some errors may be tolerated but should be minimised.

**3. 低有效性 Low Validity：**主要用于激发思路、趋势分析或创新，例如一般性用户反馈。Low validity may be acceptable for inspiration, trend analysis, or innovation, such as general user feedback.

#### 1.9 如何有效查找信息 How to Find Information Effectively

**1. 明确需求 Define Requirements：**确定自己需要查找什么信息。Clarify what information you are looking for.

**2. 选择正确来源 Choose the Right Sources：**根据信息用途选择一手、二手或三手来源。Select primary, secondary, or tertiary sources according to the intended use.

**3. 使用有效搜索策略 Use Effective Search Strategies：**使用关键词和 AND、OR、NOT 等布尔运算符。Use keywords and Boolean operators such as AND, OR, and NOT.

**4. 评估可信度 Evaluate Credibility：**使用 CRAAP 测试评价信息质量。Use the CRAAP test to evaluate information quality.

**5. 组织和保存信息 Organise and Store Information：**使用 Zotero、Mendeley 或 EndNote 等参考文献管理工具，并保存结构化笔记。Use reference managers such as Zotero, Mendeley, or EndNote and maintain structured notes.

**6. 应用与沟通 Apply and Communicate：**将原始信息转化为具有洞察力的报告。Transform raw information into insightful reports.

#### 1.10 CRAAP 信息评价测试 CRAAP Test

CRAAP 代表五项信息评价标准。CRAAP represents five criteria for evaluating information.

**1. 时效性 Currency：**信息是否为最新内容？什么时候发布或更新？Is the information current? When was it published or updated?

**2. 相关性 Relevance：**信息是否与当前问题直接相关？Is the information directly relevant to the problem?

**3. 权威性 Authority：**作者、出版者或组织是谁？他们是否是受到认可的专家？Who is the author, publisher, or organisation? Are they recognised experts?

**4. 准确性 Accuracy：**信息是否得到证据、参考资料或数据支持？Is the information supported by evidence, references, or data?

**5. 目的 Purpose：**信息为什么被创建？是否存在偏见？Why was the information created? Does it contain bias?

### 2. 引用信息来源 Citing and Referencing Information Sources

引用是对自己使用的信息来源给予承认的方式。Citation is the process of giving credit to the sources of information used.

在 IT 专业实践和学术研究中，引用具有三项重要作用。In IT professional practice and academic research, citation has three important functions.

**1. 承认作者身份 Acknowledge Authorship：**注明原作者并避免抄袭。It gives credit to the original author and prevents plagiarism.

**2. 支持可信度 Support Credibility：**表明观点和信息有证据支持。It demonstrates that claims and information are supported by evidence.

**3. 允许验证 Enable Verification：**使其他人能够查找和检查原始来源。It allows others to locate and examine the original source.

**常见引用格式 Common Citation Styles：**APA 常用于 IT 和社会科学，本课程作业采用 APA 第七版；Harvard 广泛应用于不同学科；IEEE 常用于工程、计算机科学和 IT 标准。APA is commonly used in IT and social sciences, and APA 7 is used for course assignments. Harvard is widely used across disciplines, while IEEE is common in engineering, computer science, and IT standards.

### 3. IT 研究 IT Research

#### 3.1 什么是研究 What Is Research?

研究是一个系统地收集、分析和解释信息或数据的过程，其目的是回答问题、解决问题或产生新知识。Research is a systematic process of collecting, analysing, and interpreting information or data to answer questions, solve problems, or generate new knowledge.

#### 3.2 研究的关键特征 Key Characteristics of Research

**1. 系统性 Systematic：**遵循清晰的方法或流程。Research follows a clear methodology or process.

**2. 客观性 Objective：**在不受个人偏见影响的情况下寻求事实或有效结果。Research seeks truth or effective outcomes without personal bias.

**3. 基于证据 Evidence-Based：**依赖数据、实验或可靠来源。Research relies on data, experiments, or reliable sources.

**4. 可复制性 Replicable：**其他人应当能够遵循相同过程并获得相似结果。Others should be able to follow the process and obtain similar results.

**5. 创新性 Innovative：**研究通常旨在发现新的见解或改进现有方法。Research often aims to discover new insights or improve existing approaches.

#### 3.3 研究类型 Types of Research

**1. 基础研究 Pure Research：**扩展理论知识，例如研究算法或人工智能模型，不一定立即产生实际应用。It expands theoretical knowledge, such as algorithms and AI models, without necessarily producing an immediate practical application.

**2. 应用研究 Applied Research：**解决实际问题，例如开发人力资源管理系统。It solves practical problems, such as developing a Human Resource Management System.

**3. 定性研究 Qualitative Research：**关注非数字化的观点和体验，例如用户体验研究。It focuses on non-numerical insights and experiences, such as user-experience research.

**4. 定量研究 Quantitative Research：**使用可测量的数值数据，例如系统性能基准测试。It uses measurable numerical data, such as system-performance benchmarks.

#### 3.4 为什么研究在 IT 中很重要 Why Is Research Important in IT?

**1. 创新与进步 Innovation and Progress：**IT 领域中的人工智能、云计算、网络安全、物联网和区块链发展迅速，研究能够推动新技术、方法和工具的发展。IT fields such as AI, cloud computing, cybersecurity, IoT, and blockchain evolve rapidly, and research drives new technologies, methods, and tools.

**2. 问题解决 Problem-Solving：**研究提供系统分析和解决复杂问题的方法。Research provides systematic methods for analysing and solving complex problems.

**3. 循证决策 Evidence-Based Decision-Making：**研究确保 IT 战略建立在事实、数据和证据之上。Research ensures that IT strategies are based on facts, data, and evidence.

**4. 改善专业实践 Improving Professional Practice：**研究可以识别最佳实践、标准和方法论。Research identifies best practices, standards, and methodologies.

#### 3.5 一手研究与二手研究 Primary and Secondary Research

**一手研究 Primary Research：**直接从来源收集原始的第一手数据，目的是回答特定研究问题。数据来源包括调查、访谈、观察、实验和系统日志。其优势是内容能够针对具体问题并保持最新，局限是耗时且成本较高。Primary research collects original, first-hand data directly from the source to answer specific research questions. Sources include surveys, interviews, observations, experiments, and system logs. It is tailored and current but can be time-consuming and costly.

**二手研究 Secondary Research：**使用其他人已经收集的数据，以获得背景信息并支持分析。资料来源包括书籍、学术期刊文章、行业报告和网站。其优势是节省时间和资源并提供更广泛的视角，局限是信息可能不完全相关或不够及时。Secondary research uses existing data collected by others to gather background information and support analysis. Sources include books, journal articles, industry reports, and websites. It saves time and resources and provides a broader perspective, but may not be fully relevant or current.

#### 3.6 为什么统计学对 IT 研究很重要 Why Are Statistics Important for IT Research?

**1. 理解数据 Understanding Data：**IT 研究通常涉及大量数据，统计学提供分析、总结和解释这些数据的工具。IT research often involves large amounts of data, and statistics provides tools for analysing, summarising, and interpreting it.

**2. 将数据转化为信息 Transforming Data into Information：**原始数据只有经过分析才能形成信息，统计方法能够从原始记录中识别模式和洞察。Raw data becomes information only after analysis, and statistics identifies patterns and insights within raw records.

**3. 支持循证决策 Evidence-Based Decision-Making：**系统设计决策需要证据支持，统计检验能够确认研究结果是否有效且可靠。System-design decisions should be supported by evidence, and statistical tests help determine whether findings are valid and reliable.

#### 3.7 统计学的重要概念 Important Concepts in Statistics

**总体 Population：**研究对象、人员或数据的完整集合。The complete set of items, people, or data being studied.

**样本 Sample：**从总体中选择、用于推断总体特征的子集。A subset of the population used to make inferences.

**定量变量 Quantitative Variable：**可以测量并用数值表示的变量。A variable that can be measured and expressed numerically.

**定性变量 Qualitative Variable：**描述类别或特征的非数值变量。A non-numerical variable that describes categories or qualities.

**描述性统计 Descriptive Statistics：**使用均值、中位数和图形等方式总结数据。It summarises data using measures and displays such as the mean, median, and charts.

**推断性统计 Inferential Statistics：**根据样本数据对总体进行预测或得出结论。It uses sample data to make predictions or draw conclusions about a population.

**均值 Mean：**全部数值的平均值。The average value.

**中位数 Median：**排序后位于中间位置的数值。The middle value in an ordered dataset.

**众数 Mode：**出现次数最多的数值。The most frequently occurring value.

**极差 Range：**最大值与最小值之间的差。The difference between the maximum and minimum values.

**方差与标准差 Variance and Standard Deviation：**衡量数据点偏离均值的程度。They measure how far data points deviate from the mean.

### 4. IT 项目估算 Estimation in IT Projects

#### 4.1 什么是估算 What Is Estimation?

估算是在实际工作开始前，预测完成项目或任务所需工作量、时间、成本和资源的过程。Estimation is the process of predicting the effort, time, cost, and resources required to complete a project or task before the actual work begins.

在 IT 中，估算经常用于软件开发、系统实施、测试和基础设施建设。In IT, estimation is commonly used in software development, system implementation, testing, and infrastructure setup.

#### 4.2 估算在 IT 项目中的重要性 Importance of Estimation in IT Projects

**1. 项目规划与排期 Project Planning and Scheduling：**准确估算使项目经理能够制定有效时间表；缺少估算可能导致延误和瓶颈。Accurate estimates enable project managers to plan timelines effectively, while missing estimates can create delays and bottlenecks.

**2. 预算与成本控制 Budget and Cost Control：**组织需要预测成本以获得项目审批和资金，估算能够避免财务资源分配过多或不足。Organisations need cost forecasts for approval and funding, and estimation prevents financial resources from being over- or under-allocated.

**3. 利益相关者期望 Stakeholder Expectations：**估算有助于为客户、发起人和团队设定现实期限，并通过透明度建立信任。Estimation sets realistic deadlines for clients, sponsors, and team members and builds trust through transparency.

**4. 资源管理 Resource Management：**估算确保人力和技术资源得到有效分配。Estimation ensures that human and technical resources are allocated effectively.

**5. 风险管理 Risk Management：**估算可以尽早识别潜在超支，使团队能够制定应急计划。Estimation identifies possible overruns early and enables teams to develop contingency plans.

**6. 绩效衡量 Performance Measurement：**项目实际表现可以与估算结果进行比较。Actual project performance can be compared with the estimates.

#### 4.3 项目估算的五个步骤 Five Steps of Project Estimation

**1. 确定项目规模 Determine Project Size：**定义项目范围、功能和复杂程度，可以使用功能点、故事点或代码行数衡量。Define the project scope, features, and complexity using measures such as function points, story points, or lines of code.

**2. 确定所需工作量 Determine Required Effort：**工作量是完成项目需要投入的全部劳动，通常以人时、人日或人月衡量。Effort is the total work required to complete the project and is usually measured in person-hours, person-days, or person-months.

**3. 确定所需资源 Decide Required Resources：**资源包括人力、技术和财务资源，具体分配取决于技能要求和资源可用性。Resources include human, technical, and financial resources, with allocation depending on required skills and availability.

**4. 计算持续时间 Calculate Duration：**持续时间是完成项目需要经过的日历时间，工作量与持续时间并不相同。Duration is the calendar time required to complete the project, and it is different from effort.

**5. 计算成本 Calculate Cost：**成本由工作量、资源费率和管理费用共同决定，并用于预算编制和客户谈判。Cost is determined by effort, resource rates, and overheads and supports budgeting and client negotiations.

项目成本 Project Cost=(工作量 Effort×资源费率 Resource Rate)+管理费用 Overheads

#### 4.4 项目估算的五种方法 Five Methods of Project Estimation

**1. 功能点分析 Function Point Analysis, FPA：**根据系统必须执行的功能数量估算项目规模，每项功能根据简单、一般或复杂程度赋予不同权重。它适合需求定义清晰的软件项目。It estimates project size by counting the functions a system must perform and weighting each function as simple, average, or complex. It is suitable for software projects with well-defined requirements.

**2. 算法成本模型 Algorithmic Cost Models：**使用基于历史数据的数学模型进行估算，最著名的例子是构造性成本模型 COCOMO。它常用于代码规模可测量的大型项目。These use mathematical models based on historical data. The best-known example is COCOMO, the Constructive Cost Model, which is often used for large projects with measurable code size.

**3. 专家判断 Expert Judgement：**由经验丰富的 IT 专业人员根据过去经验进行估算，适合需求仍不确定的项目早期。虽然具有主观性，但熟悉相关领域的专家通常可以提供现实估算。Experienced IT professionals estimate the project based on past experience. It is suitable for early-stage projects with uncertain requirements and, despite being subjective, can be realistic when experts understand the domain.

**4. 类比估算 Analogous Estimation：**将当前项目与过去的类似项目进行比较，再根据差异调整结果。它适合拥有可作为基准的历史项目时使用。It compares the current project with similar past projects and adjusts for differences. It is most useful when historical projects are available as benchmarks.

**5. 分解汇总法 Sum of Parts：**将项目分解为任务或子任务，分别估算每项任务，再汇总为整体估算。它适合任务定义清晰的详细项目规划。It breaks the project into tasks or subtasks, estimates each separately, and then sums the results. It is suitable for detailed planning when tasks are well defined.

#### 4.5 估算方法的局限和偏差 Limitations and Biases of Estimation Methods

**专家判断 Expert Judgement：**局限是高度依赖个人经验，容易受到主观乐观或悲观偏差影响。It depends heavily on individual experience and may be affected by subjective optimism or pessimism.

**类比估算 Analogous Estimation：**局限是必须拥有真正可比较的高质量历史项目数据，容易过度依赖项目之间的表面相似性。It requires reliable and genuinely comparable historical data and may over-rely on apparent similarities.

**功能点分析 Function Point Analysis：**局限是可能非常耗时，估算结果也取决于估算人员对系统复杂度的解释。It can be very time-consuming, and the result depends on the estimator’s interpretation of system complexity.

**算法成本模型 Algorithmic Cost Models：**局限是依赖模型假设和历史数据校准。如果输入数据质量差，输出结果也会不可靠，即“垃圾输入，垃圾输出”。They rely on assumptions and calibrated historical data. Poor-quality input produces unreliable output—“garbage in, garbage out.”

**分解汇总法 Sum of Parts：**局限是可能重复计算工作量或忽略隐藏依赖关系，同时容易受到锚定偏差影响。It may double-count effort or overlook hidden dependencies and can be affected by anchoring bias.

### 1. IT 治理的含义与作用 Meaning and Role of IT Governance

### 10. COBIT 治理与管理框架 COBIT Governance and Management Framework

#### 10.1 COBIT 基本信息 COBIT Overview

COBIT 的全称是“信息及相关技术控制目标”，由 ISACA 于 1996 年开发，是全球认可的企业 IT 治理和管理框架。COBIT stands for Control Objectives for Information and Related Technologies. It was developed by ISACA in 1996 and is a globally recognised framework for enterprise IT governance and management.

#### 10.2 COBIT 的结构 COBIT Structure

COBIT 将活动分为治理目标和管理目标。COBIT separates activities into governance objectives and management objectives.

**治理目标 Governance Objectives：**遵循“评估—指导—监控”模型。Governance objectives follow the Evaluate–Direct–Monitor model.

评估 Evaluate→指导 Direct→监控 Monitor

**管理目标 Management Objectives：**遵循“规划—构建—运行—监控”模型。Management objectives follow the Plan–Build–Run–Monitor model.

规划 Plan→构建 Build→运行 Run→监控 Monitor

#### 10.3 COBIT 的治理目标 Governance Objectives

治理目标关注对 IT 的指导和监督，并回答：“我们是否在做正确的事？”Governance objectives concern the direction and oversight of IT and ask: “Are we doing the right things?”

治理通常由董事会、高级管理人员、CIO 和指导委员会负责。Governance is generally owned by the board, senior executives, the CIO, and steering committees.

**1. 评估 Evaluate：**评估利益相关者需求、业务目标、风险和合规要求。Assess stakeholder needs, business objectives, risks, and compliance requirements.

**2. 指导 Direct：**确定优先事项、作出治理决策并分配资源。Set priorities, make governance decisions, and allocate resources.

**3. 监控 Monitor：**持续检查绩效、合规状况和利益相关者满意度。Continuously review performance, compliance, and stakeholder satisfaction.

#### 10.4 COBIT 的管理目标 Management Objectives

管理目标关注 IT 的日常运营，并回答：“我们是否以正确的方式做事？”Management objectives concern the daily operation of IT and ask: “Are we doing things the right way?”

管理通常由 IT 经理、项目经理和运维团队负责。Management is generally owned by IT managers, project managers, and operations teams.

**1. 规划 Plan：**包括战略制定、预算、企业架构和项目规划。It includes strategy, budgeting, enterprise architecture, and project planning.

**2. 构建 Build：**包括开发解决方案、采购基础设施和配置系统。It includes developing solutions, acquiring infrastructure, and configuring systems.

**3. 运行 Run：**包括服务运营、服务台、事件管理和变更管理。It includes operating services, running service desks, managing incidents, and controlling changes.

**4. 监控 Monitor：**包括绩效评估、安全监控、服务可用性检查和风险监控。It includes evaluating performance, monitoring security, checking service availability, and reviewing risks.

#### 10.5 COBIT 的演变历史 Evolution of COBIT

**COBIT 1：**主要关注 IT 审计的控制目标。It focused primarily on control objectives for IT auditing.

**COBIT 2：**增加了更广泛的 IT 管理和治理要素。It added a broader range of IT management and governance elements.

**COBIT 3：**进一步强调与业务目标对齐和风险管理。It strengthened alignment with business objectives and risk management.

**COBIT 4：**整合治理与管理，并与 ITIL、ISO 等其他框架结合。It integrated governance and management and connected with frameworks such as ITIL and ISO.

**COBIT 5：**提出五项关键治理原则。It introduced five key governance principles.

**COBIT 2019：**在原有原则基础上增加第六项原则，并引入设计因素。It added a sixth principle and introduced design factors.

#### 10.6 COBIT 5 的五项关键治理原则 Five Key Governance Principles of COBIT 5

**1. 满足利益相关者需求 Meeting Stakeholder Needs：**确保 IT 目标与业务目标和利益相关者期望保持一致。例如，大学可以使 IT 服务与改善学生体验的目标保持一致。Ensure that IT objectives align with business objectives and stakeholder expectations. For example, a university can align its IT services with student-experience goals.

**2. 端到端覆盖整个企业 Covering the Enterprise End to End：**治理应覆盖整个组织，而不只是 IT 部门。例如，银行可以将 IT 风险纳入企业风险管理框架。Governance should apply across the entire organisation rather than only within the IT department. For example, a bank can incorporate IT risk into its enterprise risk-management framework.

**3. 应用单一集成框架 Applying a Single Integrated Framework：**COBIT 5 可以与 ITIL、ISO/IEC 27001 和 TOGAF 等标准及框架整合。例如，医院可以结合使用 COBIT 5 和 ISO 27001，以保证安全合规。COBIT 5 can integrate with standards and frameworks such as ITIL, ISO/IEC 27001, and TOGAF. For example, a hospital can use COBIT 5 together with ISO 27001 to support security compliance.

### 11. IT 服务管理与 ITIL 4 IT Service Management and ITIL 4

#### 11.1 什么是 ITSM What Is ITSM?

IT 服务管理是一门将 IT 作为一组服务进行管理的学科和实践，目标是为内部或外部客户创造价值。IT Service Management is the discipline and practice of managing IT as a set of services that provide value to internal or external customers.

ITSM 主要关注三个方面。ITSM focuses on three main areas.

**1. 流程 Process：**IT 服务应当如何交付。How IT services should be delivered.

**2. 人员 People：**谁使用、支持和管理 IT 服务。Who uses, supports, and manages IT services.

**3. 服务 Service：**IT 为业务提供什么成果，以及用户应如何使用这些成果。What outcomes IT provides to the business and how users should use them.

#### 11.2 COBIT 与 ITIL 的区别 Difference Between COBIT and ITIL

**COBIT：**属于企业 IT 治理与管理框架，重点说明为了使 IT 与业务目标对齐，组织“应该做什么”，由 ISACA 维护。COBIT is a governance and management framework for enterprise IT. It focuses on what should be done to align IT with business objectives and is maintained by ISACA.

**ITIL：**属于 IT 服务管理最佳实践框架，重点说明 IT 服务“应该如何交付和管理”，由 AXELOS 维护。ITIL is a best-practice framework for IT Service Management. It focuses on how IT services should be delivered and managed and is maintained by AXELOS.

#### 11.3 ITSM 的发展历史 History of ITSM

**20 世纪 60 年代 1960s：**IT 主要以硬件为中心，支持工作通常是临时进行的，没有正式流程。IT was primarily hardware-centred, support was ad hoc, and formal processes were generally absent.

**20 世纪 80 年代 1980s：**组织开始意识到 IT 服务必须保持可靠和标准化，英国政府创建了 ITIL v1。Organisations recognised that IT needed to be reliable and standardised, and the UK government created ITIL v1.

**20 世纪 90 年代 1990s：**ITIL v2 成为全球 ITSM 最佳实践标准。ITIL v2 became a global standard for ITSM best practices.

**21 世纪初 2000s：**ITIL v3 引入服务生命周期模型，ITSM 从单纯的 IT 支持发展为与业务对齐的 IT 服务交付。ITIL v3 introduced a service-lifecycle model, and ITSM expanded beyond IT support into business-aligned service delivery.

**20 世纪 10 年代 2010s：**ITIL 4 整合敏捷原则，使 ITSM 更加灵活。ITIL 4 integrated Agile principles to make ITSM more flexible.

**当前 Today：**ITSM 重点关注云服务、自动化、人工智能和用户体验。ITSM now focuses on cloud services, automation, artificial intelligence, and user experience.

#### 11.4 ITIL v3 服务生命周期 ITIL v3 Service Lifecycle

**1. 服务战略 Service Strategy：**将 IT 定义为能够创造价值的战略服务提供者。Define IT as a strategic service provider that delivers value.

**2. 服务设计 Service Design：**设计能够满足业务需求的 IT 服务和流程。Design IT services and processes that meet business requirements.

**3. 服务转换 Service Transition：**安全地构建、测试和部署新的或变更后的 IT 服务。Build, test, and deploy new or changed IT services securely.

**4. 服务运营 Service Operation：**高效运行并支持 IT 服务。Operate and support IT services efficiently.

**5. 持续服务改进 Continual Service Improvement：**根据反馈和绩效指标持续衡量并改进服务。Continuously measure and improve services using feedback and performance metrics.

#### 11.5 ITIL 4 的特点 Features of ITIL 4

ITIL 4 是 AXELOS 于 2019 年发布的最新版本，强调价值驱动、灵活性和现代数字环境。ITIL 4 is the latest version released by AXELOS in 2019 and is value-driven, flexible, and designed for modern digital environments.

它同时整合了敏捷、精益和 DevOps 的思想与实践。It incorporates Agile, Lean, and DevOps concepts and practices.

#### 11.6 ITIL 4 服务价值系统 ITIL 4 Service Value System, SVS

ITIL 4 的服务价值系统包含五个主要部分。The ITIL 4 Service Value System consists of five main components.

**1. 指导原则 Guiding Principles：**为组织决策和行动提供通用建议。General recommendations that guide organisational decisions and actions.

**2. 治理 Governance：**负责监督、指导和合规。It provides oversight, direction, and compliance.

**3. 服务价值链 Service Value Chain, SVC：**用于创造和交付价值的运营模式。An operating model for creating and delivering value.

**4. 实践 Practices：**34 项 ITIL 实践取代了旧版本中的“流程”概念。Thirty-four ITIL practices replace the older concept of fixed processes.

**5. 持续改进 Continual Improvement：**持续改善服务、实践和价值交付。It continually improves services, practices, and value delivery.

#### 11.7 ITIL 4 的七项指导原则 Seven Guiding Principles of ITIL 4

**1. 聚焦价值 Focus on Value：**所有活动都必须为利益相关者提供价值。Every activity should contribute value to stakeholders.

**2. 从现状开始 Start Where You Are：**有效利用组织现有的资源、流程和能力，而不是不加评估地全部重新开始。Use existing resources, processes, and capabilities effectively rather than replacing everything without assessment.

**3. 通过反馈迭代推进 Progress Iteratively with Feedback：**规模较小的渐进式改进通常优于规模较大且风险较高的变更。Small, incremental improvements are generally safer and more effective than large, risky changes.

**4. 协作并提高可见性 Collaborate and Promote Visibility：**通过团队合作和信息透明提高决策与服务质量。Improve decisions and service quality through collaboration and transparency.

**5. 整体思考和工作 Think and Work Holistically：**考虑组织、人员、技术、合作伙伴和服务的所有相关方面。Consider all relevant aspects of the organisation, people, technology, partners, and services.

**6. 保持简单实用 Keep It Simple and Practical：**避免不必要的复杂性，只保留能够创造价值的步骤。Avoid unnecessary complexity and retain only the steps that create value.

**7. 优化并自动化 Optimise and Automate：**先改进流程，再合理使用技术实现自动化。Improve processes first and then use technology appropriately to automate them.

#### 11.8 ITIL 4 服务价值链 ITIL 4 Service Value Chain

服务价值链包含六项关键活动。The Service Value Chain consists of six key activities.

**1. 规划 Plan：**确保组织对愿景、当前状态和未来方向形成共同理解。Ensure a shared understanding of the vision, current state, and future direction.

**2. 改进 Improve：**持续改进服务、实践和流程。Continually improve services, practices, and processes.

**3. 互动 Engage：**与利益相关者互动并理解其需求。Engage with stakeholders and understand their needs.

**6. 交付与支持 Deliver and Support：**运行服务并为用户提供支持。Operate services and support users.

#### 11.9 ITSM、COBIT 与 ITIL 的区别 Difference Between ITSM, COBIT and ITIL

**ITSM：**属于服务管理学科和实践，关注如何将 IT 作为服务交付，范围主要是服务管理，层次主要涉及运营和战术。ITSM is a service-management discipline and practice. It focuses on delivering IT as a service, covers service management, and operates mainly at operational and tactical levels.

**ITIL：**属于最佳实践框架，关注如何实施 ITSM 流程和实践，范围覆盖服务生命周期，层次主要涉及运营和战术。ITIL is a best-practice framework focused on implementing ITSM processes and practices. It covers the service lifecycle and operates mainly at operational and tactical levels.

Precautions: Install and update antivirus/anti-malware software; keep systems and software patched and updated; educating users on phishing and safe browsing practices; Use firewalls and intrusion detection systems; Regularly and securely back up critical data; Limit unnecessary administrative permissions

Cybercrime #6 - Phishing and Social Engineering Social engineering: manipulating or deceiving people into revealing confidential information or performing actions that compromise security; Phishing: A type of social engineering that uses emails, messages, or websites to trick users into providing sensitive data; Key idea: attackers exploit human psychology, not just technical vulnerabilities; Preventive Measures: User Awareness and Training: Teach employees to recognize suspicious emails, links, and messages; Multi-factor authentication (MFA): Prevents stolen credentials from being sufficient to access accounts; Email and web filters: Block malicious content before it reaches users

Cybercrime #7 - DDoS Attacks: Definition: Distributed denial-of-service attacks aim to overwhelm a target (server, service, network) with traffic or requests from many distributed sources, making them inaccessible to legitimate users; The goal is destruction rather than data theft (although DDoS is sometimes used as a smokescreen for other attacks). Preventive measures: Traffic scrubbing/scrubbing center (third-party DDoS mitigation service); rate limiting and upstream filtering in collaboration with ISPs; autoscale with discreet limits; load balancers and redundancy across regions; Centralized logging, SIEM, and real-time traffic monitoring

Cybercrime #8 - Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs)/Advanced Persistent Threats: Definition: Long-term and targeted cyberattacks in which an intruder gains unauthorized access to a network and remains undetected for an extended period of time; APTs often target high-value assets such as intellectual property, financial data, or government secrets. Preventive measures: Continuous network monitoring, anomaly detection, endpoint detection; Close known security vulnerabilities that APTs may exploit; detailed plans for detecting, containment, and remediation of intrusions; Deploy least privilege, network segmentation, and multi-factor authentication

1. Data Lifecycle生命周期:Introduction: The data lifecycle is a fundamental concept in information security and data governance. It shows how data is created, used, stored, and ultimately disposed of; Understanding this is crucial for IT professionals as security controls must be applied at every stage; Data is not static - it goes through stages. Common models have 6-7 stages (some frameworks are slightly different).

a) Explain how BI architecture components (data sources, ETL, warehouse, OLAP) work together.（3 marks）
