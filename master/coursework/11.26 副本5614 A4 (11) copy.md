### 第一部分：信息量度与熵的理论基础

1.1 信息的本质理解Understanding the Nature of Information

经典案例分析："狗咬人"：概率高 → 信息量小 → 不构成新闻."人咬狗"：概率极低 → 信息量大 → 头版新闻Classic Case Analysis:"Dog bites man": High probability → Low information content → Not newsworthy."Man bites dog": Extremely low probability → High information content → Front-page news

核心原理：信息量与事件发生概率成反比关系.数学表达：I(x) = -log P(x).概率越小，不确定性越大，信息量越大Core Principle: Information content is inversely related to the probability of occurrence.Mathematical expression: I(x) = -log P(x).Lower probability → Higher uncertainty → Greater information content

1.2 熵的深入理解In-depth Understanding of Entropy

定义与特性：熵是数据科学和信息论中的基本概念;用于量化数据集中的不确定性或随机性程度;广泛应用于机器学习决策树算法（ID3, C4.5）Definition and Characteristics:Entropy is a fundamental concept in data science and information theory;Used to quantify the degree of uncertainty or randomness in datasets;Widely applied in machine learning decision tree algorithms (ID3, C4.5)

数学公式：H(D) = -Σ P(y) log₂ P(y).其中：H(D)：数据集D的熵值.P(y)：类别y在数据集中出现的概率.求和覆盖所有可能的类别

Mathematical Formula:H(D) = -Σ P(y) log₂ P(y).Where:H(D): Entropy value of dataset D.P(y): Probability of class y appearing in the dataset.Summation covers all possible classes

直观理解：高熵：数据分布接近均匀，预测困难，不确定性大.低熵：数据分布偏向某类，预测相对容易，确定性高.Intuitive Understanding:High entropy: Data distribution approaches uniformity, prediction is difficult, high uncertainty.Low entropy: Data distribution favors certain classes, prediction is relatively easy, high certainty

1.3 熵的计算实例Entropy Calculation Examples

实例1：水果分类.数据集：[苹果, 苹果, 橙子, 苹果, 橙子, 苹果, 橙子, 橙子, 苹果, 苹果]

总计：6个苹果，4个橙子.概率计算：P(苹果) = 6/10 = 0.6.P(橙子) = 4/10 = 0.4.熵值计算：H(D) = -(0.6 × log₂(0.6) + 0.4 × log₂(0.4))     = -(0.6 × (-0.737) + 0.4 × (-1.322))

= -(-0.442 + -0.529)     = 0.971

### 第三部分：差分隐私理论与机制Differential Privacy Theory and Mechanisms

3.1 差分隐私的核心思想Core Concepts of Differential Privacy

传统方法的局限性Limitations of Traditional Methods:①匿名化：可能被反匿名化攻击破解②聚合：仍可能泄露个人信息③结果导向：只关注输出，忽视过程安全①Anonymization: Can be broken by de-anonymization attacks②Aggregation: May still leak personal information③Outcome-focused: Pays attention only to results and ignores process security

差分隐私的创新：Innovations of Differential Privacy:①过程导向：关注数据处理过程的隐私保护②数学严谨：提供可证明的隐私保障③动态适应：可构建动态系统，长期跟踪隐私损失①Process-oriented: Focuses on privacy during data processing②Mathematically strict: Provides provable privacy guarantees③Dynamic adaptation: Can build systems that track privacy loss over time

3.2 差分隐私的严格定义Formal Definition of Differential Privacy

数学定义： 对于相邻数据集D和D'（仅相差一个记录），如果算法M满足：

Pr[M(D) ∈ S] ≤ e^ε × Pr[M(D') ∈ S].则称M满足ε-差分隐私。

Mathematical Definition: For neighboring datasets D and D' (differing by only one record), if algorithm M satisfies:Pr[M(D) ∈ S] ≤ e^ε × Pr[M(D') ∈ S].Then M satisfies ε-differential privacy.

直观理解Intuitive Understanding:1.任何个人数据的存在与否不会显著改变查询结果2.攻击者无法确定特定个人是否在数据集中1.The presence or absence of any individual's data does not significantly change query results2.Attackers cannot determine whether a specific individual is in the dataset

3.3 差分隐私的核心组件详解Detailed Explanation of Core Components of Differential Privacy

组件1：查询敏感度（Sensitivity）定义：Δf = max |f(D) - f(D')|,其中D和D'是相邻数据集

Component 1: Query Sensitivity.Definition: Δf = max |f(D) - f(D')|,Where D and D' are neighboring datasets..实例计算：计数查询：敏感度 = 1（添加/删除一个人最多改变计数1）求和查询：敏感度 = 数值范围最大差.平均查询：敏感度 = (最大值-最小值)/n.Example Calculations:Count queries: Sensitivity = 1 (adding/removing one person changes count by at most 1)Sum queries: Sensitivity = maximum difference in value rangeAverage queries: Sensitivity = (max value - min value)/n

组件2：隐私预算（Epsilon, ε）:ε值含义：- ε = 0: 完美隐私，但无任何效用- ε → ∞: 无隐私保护，但保持完整效用- 实际应用：通常在0.1-10之间选择Component 2: Privacy Budget (Epsilon, ε):

ε value meanings:- ε = 0: Perfect privacy, but no utility- ε → ∞: No privacy protection, but maintains full utility- Practical applications: Usually chosen between 0.1-10

ε值选择指南ε Value Selection Guide:：ε < 1：强隐私保护，适用于高度敏感数据.1 ≤ ε ≤ 10：平衡保护，适用于中等敏感数据.ε > 10：弱保护，适用于低敏感或聚合数据.ε < 1: Strong privacy protection, suitable for highly sensitive data.1 ≤ ε ≤ 10: Balanced protection, suitable for moderately sensitive data.ε > 10: Weak protection, suitable for low-sensitive or aggregated data

组件3：拉普拉斯机制:噪声生成：Lap(b) ~ 拉普拉斯分布，其中 b = Δf/ε拉普拉斯分布PDF：f(x|μ,b) = (1/2b) × exp(-|x-μ|/b)其中μ=0为分布中心.Noise Generation: Lap(b) ~ Laplace distribution, where b = Δf/ε Laplace Distribution PDF: f(x|μ,b) = (1/2b) × exp(-|x-μ|/b) Where μ=0 is the distribution center

敏感度级别分类Sensitivity Level Classification:

高敏感（0.1 < ε ≤ 1）：①应用场景：医疗诊断、个人收入、精确位置信息②特点：强隐私保护，适度效用损失③代价：统计精度有所降低④适用情况：个人隐私优先的应用

中等敏感（1 < ε ≤ 5）：①应用场景：教育背景、就业信息、一般消费行为②特点：平衡的隐私-效用权衡③代价：可接受的精度损失④适用情况：大多数商业应用的推荐选择

低敏感（5 < ε ≤ 10）：①应用场景：公开统计、匿名化人口数据、聚合市场趋势②特点：基本隐私保护，高数据效用③代价：隐私保护相对较弱④适用情况：公共政策研究、市场分析

极低敏感（ε > 10）：①应用场景：已公开或高度聚合的数据②特点：最小隐私保护，最大数据效用③代价：隐私保护名义化④适用情况：合规性要求或公开研究

### 第二部分：熵与差分隐私的关系

核心概念:熵在理解和量化差分隐私提供的隐私保证中发挥着重要作用。熵是信息论中的概念，用于衡量数据集中的随机性或不确定性。在差分隐私的背景下，熵帮助评估需要向查询结果添加多少"噪声"才能达到特定的隐私级别。Entropy plays a significant role in understanding and quantifying the privacy guarantees provided by Differential Privacy. Entropy is a concept from information theory that measures the randomness or uncertainty in a dataset. In the context of Differential Privacy, entropy helps assess how much "noise" needs to be added to query results to achieve a certain level of privacy.

实际示例:高熵数据集：假设数据集包含从1到100变化很大的年龄，熵很高因为数据中有很多随机性。添加少量噪声可能就足以保护个人隐私，同时仍能计算准确的平均值。低熵数据集：如果数据集只包含几个非常接近的年龄（如40、41、42），熵很低因为随机性较少。这种情况下，可能需要添加更多噪声才能达到同样的隐私保护水平。High Entropy Dataset: Suppose the dataset contains ages that vary widely from 1 to 100. The entropy is high because there's a lot of randomness. Adding a small amount of noise might sufficiently protect privacy while allowing accurate average calculation. Low Entropy Dataset: If the dataset contains only a few ages very close to each other (e.g., 40, 41, 42), entropy is low due to less randomness. In this case, a larger amount of noise might be needed to achieve the same privacy level.

### 第三部分：隐私攻击类型

身份识别攻击:链接攻击：攻击者试图通过将看似匿名的数据与外部信息源连接来重新识别个人。例如，攻击者可能使用公共选民登记数据集来链接所谓的匿名医疗记录。推理攻击：攻击者通过分析看似无关的数据和背景知识来推断个人的敏感信息。Linkage Attack: Adversaries attempt to re-identify individuals by connecting seemingly anonymized data with external information sources. Example: An attacker might use a public voter registration dataset to link supposedly anonymous healthcare records. Inference Attack: Attackers infer sensitive information about individuals by analyzing seemingly unrelated data along with background knowledge.

位置隐私攻击:追踪攻击：攻击者通过分析位置数据来追踪个人随时间的移动，从而侵犯其隐私。去匿名化攻击：对手使用位置数据通过将其假名位置轨迹与真实世界身份连接来重新识别用户。Tracing Attack: Attackers track individuals' movements over time by analyzing location data, compromising their privacy. De-anonymization Attack: Adversaries use location data to re-identify users by connecting their pseudonymous location traces with their real-world identities.

通信隐私攻击:窃听：攻击者未经授权拦截私人通信，如电话或消息。流量分析：对手从通信模式中推断信息，如发送者-接收者关系，而无需直接拦截内容。Eavesdropping: Attackers intercept private communications, such as phone calls or messages, without authorization. Traffic Analysis: Adversaries infer information from communication patterns, such as sender-receiver relationships, without directly intercepting the content.

数据隐私攻击：成员推理攻击:成员推理攻击是一种隐私攻击，攻击者旨在确定特定数据记录是否属于用于构建机器学习模型的训练数据集。攻击利用了许多机器学习模型（特别是深度学习模型）对训练集中的数据点与非训练集数据点表现不同的事实。

Membership Inference Attack is a type of privacy attack where an adversary aims to determine whether a specific data record was part of the training dataset used to build a machine learning model. The attack exploits the fact that many machine learning models, especially deep learning models, behave differently on data points that were part of the training set compared to those that were not.

K-匿名化工作原理How k-anonymity works

分组： 第一步是根据共同特征或属性（如年龄、性别、邮政编码）将数据集中的个人分组到"桶"或"组"中。泛化： 通过降低属性精度（如用区域替代具体邮编）来减少可识别性。抑制： 若某些属性组合可能导致再识别，则完全删除这些敏感属性.实现K-匿名化： 目标是确保每个组包含至少"k"个共享相同泛化属性集的个人。这意味着组中的任何个人都无法与该组中的其他"k-1"个个人唯一区分。Grouping : The first step is to group individuals in the dataset into "buckets" or "groups" based on common characteristics or attributes (e.g., age, gender, ZIP code).Generalization: Attributes are generalized by reducing detail, such as replacing exact ZIP codes with broader regions.Suppression: Attributes that could enable re-identification are removed entirely when necessary.Achieving K-Anonymity: The goal is to ensure that each group contains at least "k" individuals who share the same set of generalized attributes. This means that any individual in a group cannot be uniquely distinguished from the other "k-1" individuals in that group.

k-匿名化中的参数"k"表示最小组大小，它根据所需的隐私保护水平选择。更高的"k"值提供更强的隐私保证，但可能导致数据的更粗糙泛化。The parameter "k" in k-anonymity represents the minimum group size, and it is chosen based on the desired level of privacy protection. 1.差分隐私Differential Privacy

2.联邦学习Federated Learning

联邦学习是一种机器学习方法，允许在多个分散设备或服务器上进行模型训练，同时保持数据本地化和私有化。它旨在通过允许数据保留在生成它的设备上，而不是将其集中在一个位置来解决隐私问题。Federated learning is a machine learning approach that allows for model training across multiple decentralized devices or servers while keeping the data localized and private. It's designed to address privacy concerns by allowing data to remain on the devices where it's generated, rather than centralizing it in one location.

联邦学习工作原理How Federated Learning Works

步骤1：设置和初始化.服务器初始化： 中央服务器启动联邦学习过程。它创建一个初始全局模型，通常随机初始化，作为训练过程的起点。客户端选择： 服务器选择一组分散的客户端或设备（如智能手机、边缘设备或本地服务器）参与联邦学习过程。

Step 1: Setup and Initialization.Server Initialization: A central server initiates the federated learning process. It creates an initial global model, often randomly initialized, that will serve as a starting point for the training process..Client Selection: The server selects a subset of decentralized clients or devices (e.g., smartphones, edge devices, or local servers) to participate in the federated learning process.

步骤2：本地模型训练.模型分发： 中央服务器将初始全局模型发送给选定的客户端，但不共享任何原始数据。.本地模型训练： 每个客户端使用其本地数据更新全局模型。客户端在其数据上本地执行模型训练迭代。.模型更新： 在每轮本地训练后，客户端只将模型更新（梯度）发送给中央服务器，而不是原始数据。Step 2: Local Model Training.Model Distribution: The central server sends the initial global model to the selected clients, but it does not share any raw data..Local Model Training: Each client uses its local data to update the global model. The clients perform model training iterations locally on their data..Model Update: After each round of local training, the clients send only the model updates (gradients) to the central server, not their raw data.

步骤3：模型聚合和改进.模型聚合： 服务器聚合来自所有参与客户端的模型更新以更新全局模型。可以使用各种聚合方法，如联邦平均、安全聚合或其他隐私保护技术。模型改进： 更新的全局模型现在包含来自所有参与客户端的知识。本地训练、模型更新和聚合的过程重复多轮，直到全局模型收敛或达到满意的性能水平。Step 3: Model Aggregation and Improvement.Model Aggregation: The server aggregates the model updates from all participating clients to update the global model. Various aggregation methods can be used, such as federated averaging, secure aggregation, or other privacy-preserving techniques..Model Improvement: The updated global model now contains knowledge from all participating clients. The process of local training, model update, and aggregation repeats for several rounds until the global model converges or reaches a satisfactory level of performance.

步骤4：模型部署和评估.全局模型部署： 一旦联邦学习过程完成，训练好的全局模型可以以集中方式部署用于推理，同时保持个别客户端数据私有。Step 4: Model Deployment and Evaluation.Global Model Deployment: Once the federated learning process is complete, the trained global model can be deployed for inference in a centralized manner while keeping the individual client data private.

### 2.1 同态加密Homomorphic Encryption同态加密可以让计算直接在加密数据上进行，这表示算法能处理加密输入，并得到加密输出，之后再解密得到最终结果；常见的同态加密方案包括部分同态加密（如 ElGamal）和完全同态加密（如 CKKS、BFV）。Homomorphic encryption allows computations to run directly on encrypted data, meaning an algorithm can operate on encrypted inputs and produce an encrypted output, which can then be decrypted to get the final result; examples include Partially Homomorphic Encryption (e.g., ElGamal) and Fully Homomorphic Encryption (e.g., CKKS, BFV).

### ①部分同态加密(PHE)Partially Homomorphic Encryption

### PHE允许对加密数据执行加法或乘法运算，但不能同时执行。这意味着特定方案只支持一种类型的操作而无需解密。ElGamal： 此方案支持乘法同态。如果您有两个加密值，您可以将它们相乘，结果解密后将是原始明文的乘积。RSA（Rivest–Shamir–Adleman）： 标准RSA加密方案也支持乘法同态。您可以将两个密文相乘，结果解密后将是原始消息的乘积。Paillier： 此方案支持加法同态，允许对加密数字执行加法运算。PHE allows either additive or multiplicative operations to be performed on encrypted data, but not both. This means a specific scheme will only support one type of operation without decryption.Examples of Partially Homomorphic Encryption:ElGamal: This scheme supports multiplicative homomorphism. If you have two encrypted values, you can multiply them together and the result, when decrypted, will be the product of the original plaintexts.RSA (Rivest–Shamir–Adleman): The standard RSA encryption scheme also supports multiplicative homomorphism. You can multiply two ciphertexts and the result, when decrypted, will be the product of the original messages.Paillier: This scheme supports additive homomorphism, which allows for addition operations to be performed on encrypted numbers.

### 例子（加法 PHE - Paillier）：如果我们加密 5 和 3，把它们的密文做同态加法并再解密，就能得到 5+3=8；Example (Additive PHE - Paillier): If we encrypt 5 and 3, add their ciphertexts homomorphically, and decrypt the result, we get 5+3=8.

### 例子（乘法 PHE - ElGamal）：如果我们加密 7 和 6，把两个密文同态相乘，解密后会得到 7×6=42；Example (Multiplicative PHE - ElGamal): If we encrypt 7 and 6 and multiply the ciphertexts homomorphically, decrypting gives 7×6=42.

### ② 完全同态加密（FHE）/ Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE)

### FHE 例子 / FHE Examples

### 例子（FHE - BFV）：加密 7 和 5 后，可以同态加法得到 12，也能同态乘法得到 35，最后解密即可得到正确结果；Example (FHE - BFV): Encrypting 7 and 5 lets you add (12) or multiply them (35) homomorphically, and decrypt at the end to get correct results.

定义 Definition:被动泄露是指因人为错误、弱安全措施或系统缺陷而在传输、静止或使用过程中无意暴露敏感信息，进而造成隐私、财务、声誉与合规风险。Passive leakage refers to the unintentional exposure of sensitive information due to human error, weak security, or system flaws, allowing data to leak in transit, at rest, or in use and leading to privacy, financial, reputational, and compliance risks.

### 数据泄露Data Leak

被数据泄露往往源于人为错误而非复杂漏洞，例如管理员错误配置 AWS S3 访问权限就可能意外将敏感数据公开，从而引发数据泄露事件。Data leaks are usually caused by human mistakes rather than sophisticated exploits, such as when an AWS S3 bucket is misconfigured and inadvertently exposes sensitive data to the public.

### 动信息泄露的后果Consequences of Passive Information Leakage

①被动信息泄露的后果可能很严重：暴露的数据可能被用来进行身份盗窃、欺诈或未授权访问②个人可能因此遭受隐私侵犯、名誉损害和经济损失③组织可能面临法律处罚、监管问题、客户信任下降和品牌受损④被动泄露还可能引发连锁反应，因为泄露的数据可能被用在后续的网络攻击或社工攻击中⑤因此，个人和组织都必须主动预防被动泄露并采取适当的隐私保护措施

①The consequences of passive information leakage can be severe: exposed data may be used for identity theft, fraud, or unauthorized access②Individuals may face privacy violations, reputational harm, and financial loss③Organizations may face legal penalties, regulatory issues, loss of customer trust, and brand damage④Passive leaks can also cause cascading risks because leaked data can support later cyberattacks or social engineering⑤Therefore, individuals and organizations must proactively prevent passive leakage and apply proper privacy protections

Under the Privacy Act 1988, “personal information” means any information or opinion about an identified or reasonably identifiable individual, whether true or not, and whether recorded in a material form or not.

### 个人信息的关键概念 / Personal Information - Key Concepts

“已识别个人”指能够在数据集中被区分或辨认的个体，不一定需要姓名等正式身份标识，并依据 OAIC（含 2024 年新像素追踪指南）强调的“数据集中可辨识性”原则来判断。An “identified individual” is someone distinguishable within a dataset—even without a name—consistent with OAIC guidance, including the 2024 tracking-pixels update, which frames identification as recognisability or uniqueness in the data.

### 什么是跟踪像素？/ What Are Tracking Pixels?

跟踪像素是一段嵌入网页的小型第三方代码，会在用户访问页面时自动向第三方服务器发送其活动数据，以用于网站分析、广告成效评估和定向广告投放。A tracking pixel is a small piece of third-party code embedded in a webpage that sends user activity data to a third-party server upon page visit, enabling traffic analytics, ad performance measurement, and targeted advertising.

### 跟踪像素与个人信息 / Tracking Pixels and Personal Information

根据 OAIC 指导，跟踪像素虽不被《隐私法》禁止，但组织必须遵守《1988 年隐私法》和 APPs，因为像素数据即使无姓名也可能在可被合理识别的情况下构成个人信息。  
According to OAIC guidance, tracking pixels are lawful but must comply with the Privacy Act 1988 and the APPs, since pixel-collected data may still be personal information if an individual is reasonably identifiable even without names.

部署跟踪像素的组织必须遵守《隐私法》和 APPs，否则将面临投诉与执法，同时在公众普遍认为在线跟踪不公平的背景下，更需记录像素使用、监控其收集与披露的数据，并确保透明度及提供退出选项。  
Organisations using tracking pixels must comply with the Privacy Act and APPs or face complaints and enforcement, and given widespread public concerns about online tracking, they must document pixel use, monitor collected and disclosed data, and ensure transparency and opt-out controls.

### 合理可识别 / Reasonably Identifiable

“合理可识别”指只要信息本身或与其他数据结合后，让有动机且具备常规资源的人能够在无需特殊努力的情况下锁定或追踪到某个个体，即便没有姓名，该人仍被视为可被合理识别“Reasonably identifiable” means an individual can be singled out or traced from the information—alone or combined with other data—by a motivated person using ordinary resources without extraordinary effort, even if the data does not include a name.

“去识别化”这个词容易产生歧义：它到底指的是仅仅移除了标识符的数据，还是指完全无法识别个人的数据？根据隐私法，只有当识别风险极低、个人不再能从数据中被合理识别时，信息才不再被视为“个人信息”。The term “de-identified” can be ambiguous: does it mean data with identifiers simply removed, or data that cannot be identified at all? ### 去识别化何时有用 / When de-identification (deID) is useful

1. 用于减少法律义务;去识别化可使组织在不触发全部个人信息义务的情况下共享数据，如医院发布不含身份信息的统计结果。De-identification allows data use or sharing without triggering full personal-information obligations, such as hospitals releasing non-identifiable statistics.

2. 降低数据安全风险;去识别化降低泄露风险，例如大学分享无法回溯到个人的调查结果。 De-identification reduces security risks, such as universities sharing survey data that cannot identify individuals.

3. 支持“隐私设计” 去识别化可作为隐私设计的一部分，如健身应用仅存储匿名活动模式。It supports Privacy by Design, such as fitness apps storing only anonymised activity patterns.

4. 支持二次用途;去识别化允许数据用于研究或分析等二次目的，如银行分析匿名交易数据研究消费趋势。It enables secondary uses like research, such as banks analysing de-identified transaction data.

5. 作为删除的替代方案;去识别化可替代删除，允许长期保留无身份风险的历史数据，如去识别化人口普查记录。It can substitute deletion by allowing long-term retention of useful de-identified datasets, such as census records.

有动力入侵者测试的基本原理 / Motivated Intruder Test Rationale即使删除了直接标识符（如姓名、地址、ID），仍然有人可能把数据与公开的信息（社交媒体、选民名册、公司注册等）结合来推断个人身份。测试假设入侵者拥有一般资源和耐心，但不具备专业的黑客或取证技能。

The core idea of the Motivated Intruder Test is that even if direct identifiers (like name, address, ID) are removed, someone could still link the dataset with publicly available information (such as social media, electoral rolls, or company registers) to identify individuals. The test assumes the intruder has ordinary resources and persistence, but no specialist hacking or forensic skills.

### 隐私风险 / Privacy Risks①身份披露：识别一个人的身份，例如识别Alice②属性披露：即使不知道某人的身份，仍然可以推断出他们的敏感属性，例如作为教师，通过使用名字可以找到他关于考试成绩的属性③个体化：你发现了一个人的特征集合，但不是他们的身份，例如在线广告针对显示特定兴趣的人①Identity disclosure: identifying a person, such as identifying Alice②Attribute disclosure: inferring someone's sensitive attributes even without knowing their identity, for example finding a teacher’s exam-score information using only a name③Individualization: discovering a set of traits about a person without knowing who they are, such as online ads targeting people who show specific interests

### 去识别化技术对比表 / De-identification Techniques Comparison

聚合（Aggregation）：指将个人记录合并为摘要统计以隐藏个体身份。示例：不显示每个学生的成绩，而是仅发布班级平均分。局限性：细节被削弱，极端值仍可能暗示特定个人.Aggregation: Combines individual records into summary statistics to hide specific identities. Example: Publishing only class averages instead of individual scores. Limitation: Loss of detail, and extreme values may still hint at individuals.抑制（Suppression）：从数据集中删除敏感或罕见值以降低识别风险。示例：移除完整出生日期，只保留出生年份。局限性：过度抑制会严重降低数据的分析价值。Suppression: Removes sensitive or rare values to reduce re-identification risk. Example: Removing full birth dates and keeping only the year. Limitation: Excessive suppression reduces the usefulness of the dataset.泛化（Generalisation）：用更宽泛的类别替换具体值以减少可识别性。示例：将“45 Red St, Smithfield”改为“Smithfield, NSW”。局限性：精度下降，可能影响研究或模型的准确性。Generalisation: Replaces precise values with broader categories to reduce identifiability. Example: Changing “45 Red St, Smithfield” to “Smithfield, NSW”. Limitation: Reduced precision may weaken research accuracy.假名化（Pseudonymisation）：用人工代码或假名替换直接标识符。示例：用“User123”替代“John Smith”。局限性：若映射密钥泄露，身份仍可被恢复。Pseudonymisation: Replaces direct identifiers with artificial codes or pseudonyms. Example: Replacing “John Smith” with “User123”. Limitation: If the mapping key is compromised, identities can still be reconstructed.扰动（Perturbation）：通过添加噪声或轻微修改数据值来掩盖真实细节。示例：将年龄从29改为28或30。局限性：噪声过多损害准确性，噪声过少提供的保护不足。Perturbation: Masks exact details by adding noise or slightly modifying values. Example: Changing age 29 to 28 or 30. Limitation: Too much noise harms accuracy; too little gives weak privacy protection.

### 假名化 / Pseudonymisation

假名化通过用加密值、哈希值、随机数或 SLK 等假名替换直接标识符，使两个含共同标识符的数据集能够被匹配，而抑制与泛化无法实现这种链接。Pseudonymisation replaces direct identifiers with pseudonyms—such as encrypted values, hashes, random numbers, or SLKs—allowing linkage between datasets with shared identifiers when suppression or generalisation cannot.

### 统计链接密钥 / Statistical Linkage Key统计链接密钥（SLK）通过从姓名、出生日期与性别的部分信息生成代码，使跨数据集匹配同一人的记录成为可能，但因属于假名化而非匿名化，在掌握编码方式与原始标识符时仍可能被重新识别。An SLK generates a code from partial identifiers like name, date of birth, and sex to link the same individual across datasets without revealing full identity, but as a pseudonymisation method— not anonymisation—it remains re-identifiable if the coding method and original identifiers are known.

### 哈希及其失败原因 / Hashing and Why it fails?

什么是哈希？哈希是一种密码学技术，将输入数据（例如姓名、ID或密码）转换为固定长度的字符串（哈希值）。特性：1.确定性 → 相同的输入始终产生相同的输出。2.单向性 → 你不能"反向"哈希来获得输入。3.小的输入变化 → 产生非常不同的输出。哈希经常用于假名化，用编码值替换标识符。What is Hashing?Hashing is a cryptographic technique that converts input data (e.g., a name, ID, or password) into a fixed-length string of characters (the hash).Example: Alice123 → SHA-256 →7c222fb2927d828af22f592134e8932480637c0d…Properties:1.Deterministic → same input always gives same output.2.One-way → you can't "reverse" a hash to get the input.3.Small changes in input → produce very different outputs.Hashing is often used in pseudonymisation to replace identifiers with coded values.

### 隐私中哈希失败的原因 / Why Hashing Fails in Privacy

确定性 = 可链接性Deterministic = Linkability

相同值总是产生相同的哈希。如果"Alice Brown"出现在多个数据集中，哈希将匹配，允许记录被链接（重新识别风险）。字典攻击:攻击者可以预先为常见的姓名、出生日期或邮政编码生成哈希。然后与数据集哈希比较以重新识别个人。示例：可以使用预计算表猜测"Smith1980"的哈希。小域问题:某些标识符（例如性别 = M/F，或出生日期 = 365天×有限年份）的范围非常有限。攻击者可以轻松地强制所有可能的值。没有真正的匿名性:哈希是假名化，而不是匿名化，因为如果攻击者有映射或可以强制破解它，链接到原始数据的联系可以被恢复。The same value always produces the same hash. If "Alice Brown" appears in multiple datasets, the hash will match, allowing records to be linked (re-identification risk).

Dictionary Attacks:Attackers can generate hashes for common names, DOBs, or postcodes in advance. Then compare with dataset hashes to re-identify individuals.Small Domain Problem:Some identifiers (e.g., sex = M/F, or DOB = 365 days × limited years) have a very limited range. Attackers can brute-force all possible values easily.No True Anonymity:Hashing is pseudonymisation, not anonymisation, because the link to the original data can be restored if the attacker has the mapping or can brute-force it.

### 扰动 / Perturbation

扰动通过对数据进行轻微修改（如加噪声、交换或四舍五入数值）来阻断个人可识别性，同时尽量保持分析价值，是一种常见的去识别化方法。Perturbation slightly modifies data—such as adding noise, swapping values, or rounding numbers—to break identifiability while preserving analytical usefulness as a de-identification technique.

如何在隐私中使用扰动：随机噪声添加：向薪资或年龄添加小的随机数。示例：年龄34 → 33或36。数据交换：交换记录之间的某些属性值。示例：在两名患者之间交换邮政编码。四舍五入/上限编码：用近似值替换精确值。示例：任何超过$200,000的收入 → 报告为"$200,000+"。

扰动也是差分隐私（DP）的基础，其中噪声被添加到查询结果（而不是原始数据），以使输出足够不确定以隐藏任何单个人的存在/缺失。How Perturbation is Used in Privacy:Random Noise Addition: Add small random numbers to salaries or ages. Example: Age 34 → 33 or 36.Data Swapping: Swap some attribute values between records. Example: Exchange postcodes between two patients.Rounding/Top-coding: Replace exact values with approximate ones. Example: Any income above $200,000 → report as "$200,000+".

Perturbation is also the foundation of Differential Privacy (DP) where noise is added to query results (not raw data) so the output is uncertain enough to hide the presence/absence of any single person.

### 扰动失败的原因 / Why Perturbation Fails

噪声过少 = 隐私风险:如果扰动很小，攻击者仍可能推断真实值。示例：薪资$51,002扰动到$51,100仍然很容易猜测。噪声过多 = 效用损失:如果添加过多噪声，数据对分析变得无用。示例：扰动考试成绩使得平均班级表现无意义。统计攻击（与DP一起使用时）:即使有差分隐私，重复查询或相关数据集也可以"平均"噪声。示例：对同一DP保护数据库进行相同查询1000次 → 噪声抵消，真实值推断。小域问题:如果属性空间很小（例如性别、邮政编码），攻击者可以通过强制扰动后的可能值。示例：邮政编码只有几百个可能值。与外部数据的链接:扰动数据通常仍然可以与外部数据集匹配。示例：轻微噪声的医疗记录仍然可以链接到公开注册表。

Too Little Noise = Privacy Risk:If the perturbation is small, attackers may still infer the real value. Too Much Noise = Utility Loss:If you add too much noise, the data becomes useless for analysis. Example: Perturbing exam scores so heavily that average class performance is meaningless.Statistical Attacks (when used with DP):Even with differential privacy, repeated queries or correlated datasets can "average out" the noise. Example: Ask the same DP-protected database the same query 1000 times → noise cancels, true value inferred.Small Domain Problem:If the attribute space is small (e.g., gender, postcode), attackers can brute-force possible values even after perturbation. Example: Postcode has only a few hundred possible values.Linkage with External Data:Perturbed data can often still be matched with external datasets. Example: Slightly noised medical records can still be linked to public registries.

### 什么是隐私影响评估？/ What is a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA)?

隐私影响评估（PIA）是一种系统性流程，用于识别并管理项目的隐私风险，以确保合规、落实隐私设计、提升透明与责任，并因可按风险水平灵活调整而适用于不同规模的高隐私风险项目。A Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) is a structured process that identifies and manages privacy risks to ensure compliance, embed privacy-by-design, demonstrate accountable governance, and—being scalable to risk—applies to projects of varying size, including those with high privacy risk.

### PIA的诊断和处方角色 / PIA as Diagnosis and Prescription

诊断工具：PIA用于识别未来项目的隐私影响。处方工具：PIA用于识别最小化负面影响并最大化正面影响的方法。重要的是要理解PIA不是：不是解决问题的"药物"（解决方案本身）不是探索性手术（审计）不是尸检（评估）PIA的目的是通过系统性识别和评估来帮助改进项目，而不是提供最终的解决方案或已经完成的事后审查。A diagnosis: a tool to identify the privacy impacts of a future project And a prescription: a tool to identify ways to minimise the negatives and maximise the positives.It is important to understand that a PIA is NOT:Not the medicine (the solution itself)Nor is it exploratory surgery (audit) or an autopsy (evaluation)The purpose of PIA is to help improve projects through systematic identification and assessment, rather than provide final solutions or post-hoc reviews of completed work.

### 什么是临界值评估？/ What is a Threshold Assessment?

临界值评估是一项初步筛查，用于在处理新的或变化的个人信息时快速判断项目是否存在高隐私风险并可能需要开展 PIA，同时识别低影响项目且无论是否继续都必须保留记录。  
A threshold assessment is an initial screening that flags whether new or changed personal-information handling may indicate high privacy risk requiring a PIA, helps identify low-impact projects, and must be documented regardless of whether a PIA follows.

### 如何使用临界值评估模板 / How to use the Threshold Assessment Template

临界值评估模板的设计目的是帮助你：1.确定你的项目是否涉及处理个人信息的新方式或改变的方式2.筛选可能指向高隐私风险项目潜力的因素.如果你的项目可能存在高隐私风险，你将需要完成PIA以更详细地考虑风险（包括潜在风险的可能性和严重性）。The template is designed to help you:1.Identify whether your project involves new or changed ways of handling personal information, and 2.Screen for factors that may point to the potential for a high privacy risk project.If your project is potentially high privacy risk, you will need to complete a PIA to consider the risk in more detail (including the likelihood and severity of potential risk).

什么是高隐私风险项目？What is a high privacy risk project?

高隐私风险项目是指在个人信息处理方式上引入新的或改变的做法，可能对个人隐私造成重大影响（即使法律允许），或涉及制定／修改立法从而改变 APPs 运作，因此必须进行隐私风险筛查。A high privacy risk project is one that introduces new or changed personal-information handling likely to significantly affect individuals’ privacy—even if legally authorised—or involves new legislation that alters how the APPs operate, and therefore requires privacy-risk screening.

### 什么是"重大影响"？/ What is a 'Significant Impact'?

“重大影响”指个人因新的或变化的处理方式而可能遭受明确且不利的隐私后果，如收集敏感信息、失去数据控制、心理或经济损害、歧视或身份盗用等，并因无统一标准而需根据项目情境评估实际风险因素。A “significant impact” refers to clear and adverse privacy consequences—such as collection of new sensitive data, loss of control, psychological or financial harm, discrimination, or identity theft—with no fixed rule for assessment, requiring agencies to evaluate risk factors based on project context.

### 为什么要做 PIA？/ Why a PIA?

### PIA 的目的不是阻止项目，而是识别并降低隐私风险，确保任何对隐私的限制都合理、必要且相称，并增强透明度、合规性与公众信任。A PIA aims not to stop a project but to identify and mitigate privacy risks while ensuring any privacy limitations are reasonable, necessary, and proportionate, thereby supporting transparency, compliance, and public trust.

### 如何进行 PIA？/ How to Conduct a PIA?

### 进行PIA就是系统性地识别项目如何处理个人信息、评估其隐私风险、咨询相关利益方、提出缓解措施并记录最终判断，以确保项目在设计和执行中都符合隐私要求。Conducting a PIA involves systematically identifying how a project handles personal information, assessing privacy risks, consulting stakeholders, recommending mitigation measures, and documenting decisions to ensure privacy is embedded in design and delivery.

### 澳大利亚隐私原则（APPs）概览 / Overview of Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)

编号：APP 1｜隐私原则：个人信息的开放和透明管理 (Open and Transparent Management of Personal Information)｜含义：组织需公开透明地管理个人信息，包括说明收集什么、为何收集、如何存储，以及个人如何投诉或申请访问；Organisations must manage personal information openly and transparently, explaining what is collected, why, how it is stored, and how individuals can complain or request access.APP 2｜隐私原则：匿名性和假名性 (Anonymity and Pseudonymity)｜含义：在合理情况下应允许个人匿名或使用假名；Individuals should have the option to remain anonymous or use a pseudonym where reasonable.APP 3｜隐私原则：征求的个人信息的收集 (Collection of Solicited Personal Information)｜含义：只收集为合法业务目的所必需的信息，并以公平合法方式进行；Only collect information necessary for lawful functions, in a fair and lawful manner.APP 4｜隐私原则：处理未征求的个人信息 (Dealing with Unsolicited Personal Information)｜含义：若收到未征求的信息，应评估是否需要，若不需要应销毁或去识别化；If unsolicited information is received, assess if needed; if not, destroy or de-identify it.APP 5｜隐私原则：收集个人信息的通知 (Notification of the Collection of Personal Information)｜含义：在收集信息时通知个人其信息被收集、用途及可能共享对象；Individuals must be notified when their information is collected, including purpose and disclosure.APP 6｜隐私原则：个人信息的使用或披露 (Use or Disclosure of Personal Information)｜含义：仅可为原始目的使用或披露信息，除非获得同意或有例外；Use or disclose information only for the original purpose unless consent or an exception applies.APP 7｜隐私原则：直接营销 (Direct Marketing)｜含义：未经明确同意不得使用敏感信息进行营销；Sensitive information cannot be used for direct marketing without explicit consent.：APP 8｜隐私原则：跨境披露个人信息 (Cross-border Disclosure of Personal Information)｜含义：将数据传到海外前，必须确保接收方有等同的隐私保护；Before sending data overseas, organisations must ensure the recipient provides similar privacy protection.：APP 9｜隐私原则：政府相关标识符的使用 (Adoption, Use or Disclosure of Government Related Identifiers)｜含义：不得将政府标识符用作组织自己的标识符；Organisations must not adopt government identifiers as their own.APP 10｜隐私原则：个人信息的质量 (Quality of Personal Information)｜含义：确保信息准确、完整并保持最新；Ensure personal information is accurate, complete, and up-to-date.APP 11｜隐私原则：个人信息的安全性 (Security of Personal Information)｜含义：采取合理措施保护数据免遭滥用、丢失或未授权访问；Take reasonable steps to protect data from misuse, loss, or unauthorised access.APP 12｜隐私原则：访问个人信息 (Access to Personal Information)｜含义：个人有权访问组织持有的其个人信息；Individuals have the right to access their personal information held by an organisation.APP 13｜隐私原则：更正个人信息 (Correction of Personal Information)｜含义：个人可请求更正不准确或不完整的信息；Individuals may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.

### 信息生命周期与澳大利亚隐私原则的映射 / Mapping Information Life Cycle with Australian Privacy Principles (APP)

为了有效管理隐私风险，需要理解信息生命周期的每个阶段如何与具体的隐私原则相关联。以下是详细的映射：Collection（收集）｜关键隐私关注：收集是否合法、最少、透明？ / Is the collection lawful, minimal and transparent?｜相关APPs：APP 3（必要收集 / Necessary collection）、APP 5（收集通知 / Collection notice）、APP 4（未征求信息处理 / Unsolicited information）、APP 1（透明管理 / Transparency）Data Security（数据安全/保护）｜关键隐私关注：防止误用、干扰、丢失及未授权访问 / Prevent misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access｜相关APPs：APP 11（信息安全 / Data security）、APP 1（隐私治理 / Privacy governance）Access and Correction（访问和更正）｜关键隐私关注：个人是否能够访问并更正其信息 / Right to access and correct personal information｜相关APPs：APP 12（访问权 / Access rights）、APP 13（更正权 / Correction rights）Accuracy（准确性）｜关键隐私关注：信息是否准确、完整、最新 / Ensure information is accurate, complete and up to date｜相关APPs：APP 10（信息质量 / Information quality）Use（使用）｜关键隐私关注：是否仅用于原始目的或合法允许的用途 / Use only for the primary purpose or permitted secondary purpose｜相关APPs：APP 6（使用与披露规则 / Use & disclosure）、APP 7（直接营销 / Direct marketing）Disclosure（披露）｜关键隐私关注：披露或跨境共享是否安全、必要、合规 / Safe, necessary and lawful disclosure, including overseas transfers｜相关APPs：APP 8（跨境披露 / Cross-border disclosure）、APP 6（披露规则 / Disclosure rules）、APP 9（政府标识符限制 / Government identifier limits）Disposal（处理）｜关键隐私关注：不再需要时是否妥善销毁或去识别化 / Secure destruction or de-identification when no longer needed｜相关APPs：APP 11（销毁或去识别化要求 / Destruction or de-identification）

### 3. 收集阶段的隐私风险 / Collection Stage - Privacy Risks

1.收集唯一标识符：唯一标识符和敏感信息只能在法律要求、个人明确同意或确为合法职能必要时收集，并应尽可能提供匿名选项。2.收集通知：收集信息时要清晰告知收集内容、用途和可能的共享情况，确保个人不会对后续处理感到意外。3.必要性原则：信息只能为明确且合法的目的收集，且必须与组织核心职能相关，避免采集与服务无关的数据。4.信息质量与方式：只收集准确、最新的数据，最好直接向个人获取，并以合法、公平、透明的方式进行。1.Collection of Unique Identifiers:Unique identifiers and sensitive data should be collected only when legally required, explicitly consented to, or necessary for a lawful function, with anonymity offered where possible.2.Collection Notices:Provide clear notices explaining what is collected, how it will be used, and any sharing, ensuring individuals are not surprised by subsequent handling.3.Necessity Principle:Collect information only for a lawful and clearly defined purpose related to core functions, avoiding data unrelated to the service.4.Data Quality and Collection Method:Collect only accurate, up-to-date information, preferably directly from the individual, using lawful, fair, and transparent methods.

### 4. 数据安全阶段的隐私风险 / Data Security Stage - Privacy Risks

数据安全的重要性：Importance of Data Security:数据收集后必须依据信息安全政策采取适当安全措施，防止信息被丢失、未授权访问、误用或泄露。After data is collected, appropriate security measures must be applied under your organisation’s security policies to prevent loss, unauthorised access, misuse, or disclosure.

数据安全需要根据信息的敏感性采取技术、物理和管理三类控制措施:1技术控制： 使用密码与加密保护文件与设备，所有便携式设备需加密，数字系统必须密码保护并仅限有正当需要的人员访问，密码需安全存放且不得共享。2.物理控制： 邮寄文件时避免混淆客户信息，不要在桌面暴露敏感资料，抽屉与文件柜需上锁，带出办公室的纸质文件必须全程妥善保管。3.管理控制： 制定并执行员工数据处理政策，只在必要时间内保留信息，过期资料需安全销毁，废弃的硬盘与设备必须在处理前彻底清除数据。1.Technical controls: Use passwords and encryption for files and devices, encrypt all portable devices, password-protect digital systems with access limited to those with a legitimate need, and store passwords securely without sharing.2.Physical controls: Avoid mixing customer information in mail, keep sensitive documents off desks, lock drawers and filing cabinets, and keep physical files secure when taken outside the office.3.Administrative controls: Enforce staff data-handling policies, retain information only as necessary, securely destroy outdated records, and fully wipe hard drives and devices before disposal.

### 5.保护个人信息 / Protecting Personal Information

备份 / Backups：备份必须存放在与生产环境同等安全或加密的位置，否则会增加数据丢失或未授权访问风险；backup copies must be stored in locations as secure or encrypted as production to avoid loss or unauthorised access.

设计 / Design：系统设计应仅显示用户角色所需的数据，避免不必要的信息可见性导致误用或泄露；systems should show only role-required data to reduce unnecessary visibility and exposure risks.

虚拟数据 / Dummy Data：开发或测试不得使用真实客户数据，应使用虚拟或匿名数据以防隐私泄露；real customer data must not be used in development or testing, and dummy or anonymised data should be used to prevent privacy breaches.

使用或披露阶段的隐私风险 / Use or Disclosure Stage - Privacy Risks

使用或披露的条件:仅在以下条件下使用或共享个人信息Only use or share personal information under these conditions：①当它用于收集信息的具体目的时②当它用于与个人合理期望相一致的直接相关的二次目的时③当你拥有个人的同意时④当由法律授权或由有效的豁免涵盖时①When it is used for the specific purpose it was collected for②When it is used for a directly related secondary purpose that the individual would reasonably expect③When you have the individual’s consent④When it is authorised by law or covered by a valid exemption

### 准确性阶段的隐私风险 / Accuracy Stage - Privacy Risks

在使用或披露任何个人信息之前，你必须采取合理步骤确保它：1.与任务或目的相关2.准确且没有错误3.最新的4.完整的.这确保了你处理的信息是可靠的且适合使用或披露。Before using or disclosing any personal information, you must take reasonable steps to ensure it is:1.Relevant to the task or purpose,2.Accurate and free of errors,3.Up to date, and4.Complete.This ensures the information you handle is reliable and appropriate for use or disclosure.

### 常见的隐私风险 / Common Privacy Risks

在组织管理个人信息的过程中，存在几种常见的隐私风险需要特别关注：

1.数据匹配（Data Matching）:数据匹配是指组织将来自不同来源的个人信息链接或组合在一起的过程。这可能导致隐私风险，因为：①个人可能没有意识到他们的信息来自多个来源②链接的数据可能被用于原始目的之外的目的③被链接的信息可能被用于分析或决策，影响个人①Individuals may not realise their information comes from multiple sources②Linked data may be used for purposes beyond the original intent③Linked information may be used for analysis or decisions that affect individuals

2.用于不同目的的数据（Data Used for a Different Purpose:当收集的个人信息被用于与原始收集目的不同的目的时，这是一个重大的隐私风险。例如：①为客户服务目的收集的信息不应被用于营销②为人力资源目的收集的信息不应被用于执法③政府机构收集的信息不应被转移用于商业目的①Information collected for customer service should not be used for marketing②Information collected for HR purposes should not be used for law enforcement③Information collected by government agencies should not be transferred for commercial purposes

3.数据发布/共享（未去识别化）（Data Publishing/sharing - without de-identification）:发布或共享包含个人信息的数据而不去识别化会对个人隐私造成风险。例如：①发布包含个人姓名和联系信息的客户列表②共享包含个人标识信息的数据集，即使未进行隐式链接③在互联网上发布可能识别个人的敏感信息①Publishing customer lists containing names and contact details②Sharing datasets that include personal identifiers, even without implicit linking③Posting sensitive information online that could identify individuals

### 总结：识别隐私风险的关键步骤 / Summary: Key Steps to Identify Privacy Risks

① 了解信息生命周期 / Understand the information lifecycle： 理解个人信息在组织中的流动方式；know how personal information moves through your organisation.② 应用澳大利亚隐私原则 / Apply the APPs： 明确13项原则如何适用于项目与操作；understand how the 13 APPs apply to your activities.③ 评估生命周期阶段 / Assess lifecycle stages： 在收集、安全、访问、准确性、使用、披露和处理各阶段识别风险；consider risks at each stage of collection, security, access, accuracy, use, disclosure, and disposal.④ 识别常见风险 / Identify common risks： 关注数据匹配、误用和不适当共享等风险；look for risks such as data matching, misuse, and inappropriate sharing.⑤ 实施保护措施 / Implement safeguards： 采取技术、物理和管理控制保护个人信息；apply technical, physical, and administrative controls to protect data.⑥ 定期审查 / Review regularly： 定期检查隐私实践是否仍符合法律和组织要求；regularly review practices to ensure ongoing legal and organisational compliance.

一、差分隐私 Differential Privacy（DP）模板

1. 解释 DP 如何保护个体隐私

(1) 核心机制;差分隐私通过在发布的统计结果（例如总和、平均值、计数）上 添加精心控制的随机噪声 来保护隐私。这样一来，无论某个个体（如 [个体ID，如 P102 / U102]）的数据是否出现在数据集中，得到的统计输出的概率分布几乎相同。Differential Privacy adds carefully calibrated random noise to released statistics (e.g., sums, averages, counts), so the distribution of outputs is almost the same whether [person, e.g. P102/U102] is in the dataset or not.

(2) 对攻击者的影响;因此，攻击者即使观察或比较多次查询结果，也 无法可靠判断 该个体是否在数据集中，更无法推断出其真实的 [敏感属性，如：胆固醇、月收入、心率]。

As a result, an attacker cannot reliably tell whether [that person] is included, nor infer their true [sensitive attribute] from the outputs.

(3) 结合题干场景在本题的 [场景，如：医院共享健康数据 / 健身公司分享运动数据 / 银行发布消费统计] 中，这意味着可以在不暴露单个 [对象，如：患者 / 用户 / 客户] 具体信息的前提下，安全发布整体统计结果。

In this [scenario: hospital data sharing / fitness tracking / financial analysis], this lets us publish useful aggregate statistics without exposing any single patient’s or customer’s data.

1. 总结句;因此，DP 提供了一种对攻击者背景知识不敏感、可量化的隐私保证，使得在 [研究或分析任务，如医学研究 / 行为分析] 中可以安全地利用数据。Therefore, DP offers a strong, quantifiable privacy guarantee that is robust to background knowledge, enabling safe data use in [domain].

2. 噪声对隐私与效用的影响

(1) 隐私保护;向 [属性列，如 Age / Cholesterol / Monthly Income] 添加 [噪声类型，如 Laplace / Gaussian] 噪声，会扰动每个个体的真实取值，使得攻击者即便锁定某个个体，也难以知道其精确数值，从而降低重识别和敏感信息泄露的风险。Adding [Laplace/Gaussian] noise to [column] perturbs each person’s true value, so even if an attacker identifies someone, they still cannot learn the exact [attribute].(2) 效用损失;代价是数据精度下降、统计分布被扭曲，基于该列进行的 [分析类型，如趋势分析 / 相关性研究 / 风险分层] 会变得不那么准确和稳定。The downside is reduced accuracy: the noise distorts the distribution, making [type of analysis] less precise and reliable.(3) 结合题目目标;在本题中，研究者望分析 [分析目标，如：年龄与心脏健康 / 收入与支出关系]，噪声可能导致 [后果，如：相关系数被低估 / 回归系数偏差 / 统计显著性下降]。

Here, when analysing [goal, e.g. income–spend correlation], the added noise may cause [e.g. biased estimates, weaker correlations, loss of significance].

(4) 一句总结这体现了差分隐私中的经典权衡：隐私越强（噪声越大），数据效用越低；隐私越弱（噪声越小），分析越精确。

This illustrates the classic DP trade-off: more noise → stronger privacy but lower utility; less noise → weaker privacy but more accurate analysis.

3. ε（epsilon）的作用与权衡

(1) 小 ε;非常小的 ε 代表非常强的隐私预算：系统会加入大量噪声，使得根据输出推断任何单个个体的信息几乎不可能；但同时 [数据列，如 Workout Minutes / Work Hours/week / Monthly Spend] 的统计结果会严重失真，导致 [分析目标，如健康趋势 / 工作效率 / 经济洞察] 不再可靠。A very small ε means strong privacy and a lot of noise; it becomes almost impossible to infer an individual, but statistics on [column] become so noisy that [analysis goal] may be unreliable.(2) 大 ε;较大的 ε 则相反：只添加少量噪声，[列] 的平均值、相关性等更接近真实，利于高质量分析，但个体隐私保护变弱，攻击者更容易从输出中反推出单个记录。A larger ε adds less noise, so results on [column] are more accurate and more useful, but individual privacy is weaker.

(3) 总结句因此，选择合适的 ε 本质上是在 “隐私强度” 与 “统计可用性” 之间寻找平衡，需结合 [具体场景/法规要求] 做决策。

Thus ε is the privacy budget that balances privacy strength vs. analytic usefulness, and must be chosen in light of [context/regulatory requirements].

4. 为什么仅移除 ID 不足以保护隐私;(1) 准标识符 + 唯一性

删除 [ID列，如 User ID / Patient ID / Employee ID] 只移除了直接标识符，但 [属性组合，如 Age + Location + Exercise Time / Income + Category / Work Hours + Break Time] 往往在数据集中具有很强的唯一性，可以充当“准标识符”。Simply removing [ID column] only removes direct identifiers; combinations like [quasi-identifiers] can still uniquely pinpoint individuals.(2) 链接攻击;攻击者可以将这个“匿名”数据集与外部公开来源（如 [选民名单 / 公司花名册 / 公共统计]）进行链接，匹配这些准标识符，从而重新识别出具体个体，并获知其在数据集中的所有敏感信息。An attacker can link these quasi-identifiers with external sources (e.g. [voter list, company directory]) to re-identify people and learn their sensitive attributes.(3) DP 的必要性因此，仅移除 ID 并不能抵抗链接攻击；差分隐私通过主动添加噪声，从根本上削弱了这些精确匹配的能力，提供更强、更可证明的隐私保证。Therefore ID removal alone is insufficient; DP is needed to perturb outputs so that linkage attacks become unreliable.

二、联邦学习 Federated Learning（FL）模板

1. FL 如何保护数据隐私 / Why FL protects data privacy

① 核心机制：数据不动，模型动;联邦学习遵循“数据不动，模型动”的原则。各参与方（如 [医院 / 农业中心 / 能源公司]）的原始敏感数据（如 [CT 图像 / 植物叶片照片 / 智能电表数据]）始终保留在本地，不会被上传或集中存储到中央服务器。② 共享的不是数据，而是模型更新

每个参与方仅在本地利用自己的数据训练模型，并将 模型参数/梯度更新 发送到服务器进行聚合，而不是共享原始数据。这极大减少了敏感信息被暴露的可能性。③ 适用于隐私敏感领域;在 [医疗 / 金融 / 农业 / IoT 能源] 等对隐私要求极高、受 [GDPR / HIPAA / 澳大利亚隐私法] 监管的领域，FL 可以在不违反隐私法规的前提下实现跨组织合作训练。

① Core Principle: Data stays, model moves;Federated Learning follows the principle of “data stays local, the model moves.” Each participant (e.g., [hospital / agriculture center / energy company]) keeps its raw sensitive data ([CT scans / leaf images / smart-meter data]) locally and never uploads it to the central server

② Only model updates are shared;Each participant trains the model locally using its own data and sends only model updates (weights/gradients) to the server for aggregation. No raw data is ever shared, significantly reducing privacy risks.

③ Suitable for privacy-critical domains;FL is well-suited to privacy-sensitive areas such as [healthcare / finance / agriculture / IoT energy], where regulations like [GDPR, HIPAA, Australian Privacy Act] restrict centralising personal data.

2. 数据异质性对模型性能的影响 / How data heterogeneity affects performance

① 问题定义：非 IID（异质性数据）不同参与方在 [图像质量 / 传感器类型 / 病例分布 / 气候模式] 等方面存在差异，导致数据是非 IID（非独立同分布），这是 FL 的核心挑战之一。

② 影响机制：模型偏差与泛化下降   服务器聚合得到的全局模型可能偏向 数据量大或质量高 的节点。 对于 [数据量少/质量差] 的节点，模型泛化能力弱，预测精度下降。

③ 结合题干;例如，在本题中，[无人机图像质量不一致 / 不同地区气候差异很大 / 某些诊所病例样本少]，将导致全局模型在部分参与方上表现不佳。

① Definition: Non-IID / Heterogeneous data

Participants may differ in [image quality / sensor type / case distribution / climate patterns], creating non-IID data — a major challenge in FL.

② Impact: Model bias and reduced generalisation

The global model becomes biased toward participants with larger or higher-quality datasets..It performs poorly on under-represented or low-quality sites.

③ Link to scenario;For example, in this scenario, [low-quality drone images / different regional temperature patterns / small sample clinics] can degrade the global model’s performance for certain participants.

3. FL 与隐私法规的契合性 / FL compliance with privacy regulations (e.g., APP 6 & APP 11)

① APP 6（使用与披露）;FL 让原始数据不离开 [地区/医院/组织]，中央服务器从未“使用”或“披露”个人数据，因此符合 APP 6 对数据使用和披露的限制。

② APP 11（信息安全）;由于不需要将原始数据集中存储或跨境传输，FL 显著减少了数据泄露、窃取和滥用的风险，符合 APP 11 要求的“采取合理步骤保护个人信息安全”。

③ 总结;FL 通过“数据本地化 + 仅共享模型更新”实现了对隐私和法律监管的天然适配。

① APP 6 — Use and Disclosure;Since raw personal data never leaves [the local site/clinic/region], FL prevents unauthorised “use” or “disclosure,” supporting compliance with APP 6.

② APP 11 — Security of Personal Information;FL avoids centralised storage and cross-border transmission of raw data, greatly reducing leakage and misuse risks, aligning with APP 11’s security requirements.

③ Summary;By keeping data local and sharing only model updates, FL naturally supports privacy and regulatory compliance.

4. FL 的通信与计算挑战 / Communication & computation challenges in FL

① 通信成本高;多轮参数上传/下载带来巨大带宽需求，尤其在 [IoT 设备 / 跨区域医院 / 农业现场网络差] 情况下成为瓶颈。② 同步问题（Stragglers）;节点网络和计算速度差异大，服务器往往需要等待最慢的参与方完成，降低训练效率。③ 本地资源有限;一些参与方（如 [智能手机 / IoT 传感器 / 小型诊所设备]）计算能力弱，难以承担本地模型训练。④ 系统管理复杂;包括参与方掉线（dropout）、模型版本控制、安全聚合等，使部署和维护更具挑战性。

① High communication cost;Multiple rounds of uploading/downloading model updates require large bandwidth, especially problematic for [IoT devices / remote clinics / low-connectivity regions].② Straggler problem;The server must often wait for the slowest participant, reducing training efficiency.③ Limited local compute;Some participants (e.g., smartphones, IoT sensors, small clinics) lack sufficient computational power for local training.④ System complexity;Managing client dropout, secure aggregation, and version control adds operational difficulty in large-scale FL deployments.

三、同态加密 Homomorphic Encryption（HE）模板

1. HE 如何在加密状态下计算How HE enables computation on encrypted data

① 核心数学性质:同态加密允许在密文上直接执行 [加法 / 乘法 / 聚合等运算]，并保证：对密文做运算再解密 = 对明文做相同运算再加密的结果。

② 流程步骤（完全可套用）步骤一：加密：数据持有方（如 [医院 / 银行 / 研究机构]）将敏感数据 [填写字段，如 Height、Weight、Deposit、Loan] 加密成密文 E(x)。步骤二：密文计算：云服务器在不知道明文的情况下执行计算，例如：E(Weight) ÷ (E(Height) × E(Height))（BMI 计算）或E(Deposit) − E(Loan)（净余额）。步骤三：解密；只有拥有私钥的授权方能解密最终结果。整个过程中明文从未暴露。

③ 总结句;因此，HE 使“不可信计算方也能在加密状态下进行分析”成为可能，实现真正的可计算加密。① Core mathematical property;HE allows operations such as [addition, multiplication, aggregation] to be performed directly on ciphertexts, ensuring that:Decrypt(Compute(Ciphertext)) = Compute(Plaintext).

② Workflow;Step 1: Encryption：The data owner (e.g. hospital / bank) encrypts sensitive data；[Height, Weight, Deposit, Loan] into ciphertext E(x).;Step 2: Encrypted computation；The cloud server performs calculations without seeing plaintext, e.g.:E(Weight) ÷ (E(Height) × E(Height)) (BMI) or E(Deposit) − E(Loan) (net balance).;Step 3: Decryption；Only authorised parties with the secret key can decrypt the final output.③ Summary sentence;Thus, HE enables secure outsourced computation without revealing any raw data.

2. HE 的计算开销与性能挑战Performance and computational limitations of HE

① 同态加密的计算成本非常高，密文运算比明文慢很多倍，需要大量 CPU、内存和时间。② 密文比明文大得多，导致 存储压力增加、网络传输变慢。

③ 在 [填写场景，如医疗实时监控、实时金融交易分析] 中，由于延迟高，HE 很难满足实时需求。④ 某些 HE 方案只能支持有限的加法/乘法，复杂运算需要近似，会引入误差。① HE has extremely high computational overhead; encrypted operations are far slower and consume more CPU/RAM.

② Ciphertexts are much larger than plaintext, increasing storage and network cost.

③ In [real-time healthcare, financial fraud detection], HE’s latency makes real-time analysis difficult.

④ Many HE schemes only support limited additions/multiplications; complex operations require approximations and may introduce errors.

3. HE 相比传统加密的优势（外包计算场景）Why HE is better than traditional encryption for outsourced computation

① 传统加密必须在服务器“解密后才能计算”，导致服务器能看到全部明文 → 高隐私风险。② HE 允许服务器在从头到尾都看不到明文的情况下完成计算，即使服务器不可信也安全。③ 信任边界从“信任对方不偷看数据”变为“信任对方正确执行协议”，大幅降低攻击面。① Traditional encryption requires decryption before computation, exposing plaintext to the server and creating privacy risks.② HE allows the server to compute without ever accessing plaintext, remaining secure even if the server is untrusted.③ The trust boundary shifts from “trust the server with plaintext” to “trust the protocol,” reducing the attack surface.

4. HE 如何抵御内部人员威胁（Insider Threats）How HE protects against insider threats

内部管理员或分析师看到的只有密文，而非真实值。即使内部人员拥有系统权限，也无法查看 [金额 / 医疗值 / 行为数据] 明文。显著降低组织内部滥用敏感数据的概率。

Internal administrators and analysts only see ciphertext, not raw values.Even privileged insiders cannot read plaintext [balances / health metrics / behaviour data].Greatly reduces the likelihood of insider misuse.

5. HE 如何满足隐私法（如 APP 11）How HE supports privacy regulations (e.g., APP 11)

① HE 在存储、传输、计算三个阶段都保持加密 → 全生命周期加密。② 符合 APP 11 要求的“采取合理步骤保护个人信息安全”。③ 防止未授权访问、泄露或滥用。① HE provides end-to-end encryption across storage, transmission and computation.② Aligns with APP 11’s requirement to take reasonable steps to secure personal information.③ Prevents unauthorised access, leakage or misuse.

四、去标识化 De-identification 模板（聚合 / 抑制 / 泛化）Templates for Aggregation / Suppression / Generalization（适用于：呼吸病题、医院数据题、人口统计题、任何医疗去标识化题）

1. 聚合 Aggregation 模板;① 如何降低重识别风险;通过发布聚合后的统计摘要（如：按 [诊断 / 年龄组 / 性别] 分组的 [平均肺容量 / 计数 / 总和]），移除了所有个体级别的数据，使每个个体的信息被“淹没”在群体中。攻击者无法从这些汇总数字中定位到某一特定患者，从而显著降低重识别风险。

② 会丢失什么分析能力;但聚合也意味着丢失个体差异信息，例如无法分析 [患者间病情严重度的差异 / 个体用药反应 / 不同变量之间的微弱相关性]，可能导致重要的子群体模式被掩盖。③ 总结聚合提升隐私，但减少粒度；适合趋势分析，不适合个体或高精度分

① How aggregation reduces re-identification;Publishing aggregated summaries (e.g., average [lung capacity / oxygen level] by [diagnosis / age group]) removes all individual-level data. Each person’s information is blended with the group, preventing attackers from isolating any single patient.

② What analysis is lost;However, aggregation removes within-group variation. Analysts can no longer study [individual severity differences / personal treatment response / subtle correlations], and small subgroups may be completely hidden.

③ Summary;Aggregation strengthens privacy but sacrifices analytical granularity.

2. 抑制 Suppression 模板;① 为什么必须抑制某些字段;当某些患者拥有罕见属性（如 [极罕见疾病 / 非常年轻或年老 / 极端肺容量数值]）时，即使移除姓名，他们仍可能通过这些独特属性被重新识别。为了防止链接攻击，必须抑制 [如：精确出生日期 / 地区 / 极端值]。② 抑制带来的负面影响;抑制字段会影响某些研究，如若抑制 [患者地址 / 小区域编码]，研究人员将无法进行 [疾病地理分布分析 / 环境暴露研究]。③ 总结;抑制保护极小群体，但损害依赖被抑制变量的研究。

① Why suppression is necessary;If a patient has rare attributes (e.g., rare respiratory condition, extreme lung function values), they may be re-identified even without names. Suppressing high-risk fields such as [exact birthdate / precise location / outlier values] prevents such linkage attacks.

② Impact on analysis;Suppressing data fields can weaken analyses that depend on them. For example, removing [location codes] prevents [spatial disease clustering studies / pollution exposure analysis].

③ Summary;Suppression protects uniquely identifiable individuals but limits research relying on the suppressed fields.

3.泛化 Generalization 模板① 如何降低重识别风险;将精确值（如 出生日期、精确地址、肺容量数值）替换为更宽泛的区间（如 年龄段、邮编前两位、肺容量范围），让更多人共享同一属性，提高不确定性，使攻击者难以唯一定位某个患者。② 泛化造成的分析限制;泛化会降低数据精度。例如：

年龄段替代出生日期 → 无法做精细年龄风险分析.区域泛化 → 无法做微观地理分析.数值范围替代精确值 → 模糊趋势，降低模型准确性

③ 总结;泛化在隐私与分析精度之间取得中间平衡，但若范围过大，会严重损害研究效用。① How generalization reduces re-identification;Exact values such as birthdate, address, or lung capacity are replaced with broader categories like age ranges, partial postcodes, value intervals, increasing uncertainty and preventing attackers from pinpointing a single patient.② Analytical limitations;Generalization reduces precision. For example:Age ranges reduce ability to model fine-grained age effects  Less specific geographic codes limit spatial analysis  Value bands smooth out trends and reduce model accuracy③ Summary;Generalization balances privacy and utility but can harm analysis if ranges are too wide.

4. 三种方法的对比总结聚合：把个体数据变成群体统计 → 隐私强，但粒度最低。抑制：删除高风险字段 → 防止罕见案例被识别，但减少研究能力。

泛化：扩大字段范围 → 隐私与效用之间的折中方案

Aggregation converts individual data into group-level summaries → strong privacy, low granularity.

Suppression removes high-risk identifiers → protects rare individuals but reduces research scope.

Generalization broadens values → balances privacy and utility.

5. 万能总结句

三种去标识化方法都通过减少数据的细节程度来降低重识别风险，只是牺牲的分析能力不同：聚合牺牲最多，抑制针对性强，泛化折中。

All three techniques reduce data precision to prevent re-identification, each sacrificing utility differently:aggregation removes the most detail, suppression targets high-risk features, and generalization offers a middle ground.

1. 为什么哈希不是匿名化（只能算伪匿名化）

① 哈希是可预测的（确定性）哈希函数是确定性的：相同输入 → 始终输出相同哈希值。即使无法直接反算原文，攻击者仍可通过字典攻击推测明文。

② 字典攻击如何发生如果攻击者拥有一个可能的明文列表（如 [毕业名单 / 员工姓名列表]），他们可以：对列表中每个名字做哈希；生成“名字→哈希值”的映射；再与数据集的哈希字段比对——即可重新识别某些个体

③ 总结句因此，哈希值只是将姓名换成另一种“标识符”，并非真正匿名，属于伪匿名化。

① Hashing is deterministicGiven the same input, a hash function always produces the same output, making hashes predictable.② How dictionary attacks workAn attacker can hash a list of possible names (e.g., graduation lists, staff directories) and compare the outputs to the dataset to re-identify individuals.③ Summary sentence Hashing replaces identifiers with predictable tokens and is therefore pseudonymisation, not true anonymisation.

2. 加盐（Salting）如何防御字典攻击

① 加盐机制；在哈希前加入随机字符串（盐），形成：Hash(明文 + Salt)。

② 如何阻止攻击者；即使两个用户的名字相同，由于盐不同，最终哈希结果也完全不同，攻击者无法建立通用字典。

③ 盐必须安全存储；盐本身不是秘密，但若被攻击者获取，就可以执行“定向字典攻击”。因此必须与哈希值一起安全存储。

① Mechanism；Salting appends a random value to the input before hashing.

② Security effect；Even identical names produce different hashes, preventing precomputed dictionary tables.

③ Salt storage requirement；Salts must be stored securely; if attackers obtain them, targeted attacks may still be possible.

Question4  解释在哈希过程中加入随机盐如何防御字典攻击。展示为什么 “Sara Kim” 在使用两个不同盐时会产生不同的哈希。为什么盐必须安全存储。

盐是在哈希之前添加的随机值，例如：Hash("Sara Kim" + Salt1) → XJF82…，Hash("Sara Kim" + Salt2) → PQ9AA…。由于同一姓名在不同盐下会产生完全不同的哈希结果，攻击者无法通过预先计算的字典或彩虹表反推出真实姓名。但盐必须被安全存储，因为一旦盐被泄露，攻击者仍有可能结合哈希结果进行反向推断。

A salt is a random value added before hashing, for example: Hash("Sara Kim" + Salt1) → XJF82…, Hash("Sara Kim" + Salt2) → PQ9AA…. Different salts cause the same name to generate different hashes, preventing attackers from using precomputed dictionary or rainbow table attacks. However, salts must be stored securely, because if they are compromised, attackers may still reverse names by recomputing hashes.

Adding Laplace noise hides whether a specific student contributed to the count, protecting individual identity. However, in small majors with few students, the added noise can represent a large proportion of the total, causing significant distortions and reducing reliability.

Question 1 – 智能健身数据中的差分隐私：一家健身追踪公司通过智能手表收集用户活动数据，用于分析运动习惯和心脏健康状况。为了在向外部研究人员共享数据之前保护用户身份，采用了差分隐私（Differential Privacy，DP）技术。

Question 1 – Differential Privacy in Smart Fitness Data: A fitness tracking company collects user activity data (via smartwatches) to analyze exercise habits and heart health. To protect user identity before sharing data with external researchers, Differential Privacy (DP) is applied.

A Question：解释差分隐私如何确保加入或移除某个用户的数据（如 U102）不会显著改变发布的统计结果，例如“平均心率”。

Explain how DP ensures that including or excluding one user’s data (e.g., U102) does not significantly change the published statistics such as “average heart rate.”

差分隐私通过添加精确控制的噪声，使整体统计结果在加入或移除某个用户时几乎不变。由于输出变化极小，攻击者无法判断某个用户的数据是否被包含。

Differential privacy adds carefully controlled noise so that the overall statistics remain nearly the same even if one user is added or removed. Because the outputs barely change, attackers cannot tell whether a specific user’s data is included.

B Question：如果在“每日平均步数”字段加入噪声，这如何保护隐私？对分析运动趋势可能产生什么影响？

If noise is added to the “Average Steps per Day” column, how does it protect privacy, and what is the potential effect on analyzing exercise trends?

加入噪声可以隐藏用户的真实步数，防止通过独特活动模式被识别。然而，噪声过大可能会扭曲运动趋势，降低活动模式分析的准确性。

Adding noise hides a user’s exact step count, preventing identification through unique activity patterns. However, too much noise may distort exercise trends and reduce the accuracy of activity pattern analysis.

C Question：讨论在报告“锻炼分钟数”平均值时，由 ε（epsilon）控制的隐私与实用性之间的权衡。

Discuss the privacy–utility trade-off controlled by ε (epsilon) when reporting the “Workout Minutes” average.

较小的 ε 提供更强隐私，但噪声更大，导致平均值不够准确；较大的 ε 使结果更精确，但隐私保护较弱。ε 决定了为了保护用户要牺牲多少数据细节。

A small ε gives strong privacy but adds more noise, reducing accuracy. A large ε outputs more accurate averages but weakens privacy protection. ε determines how much detail is sacrificed to protect users.

D Question：为什么使用差分隐私比仅删除用户 ID 的“匿名化”数据集更加安全？

Why is applying DP more secure than sharing an “anonymized” dataset that excludes User IDs?

删除用户 ID 并不能防止重新识别，因为年龄、步数、心率和锻炼时间的组合仍可能唯一识别用户。差分隐私通过使输出无法显示某个个体是否参与数据来防止关联攻击。

Removing User IDs does not prevent re-identification because combinations of Age, Steps, Heart Rate, and Workout Minutes can uniquely identify users. DP prevents linkage attacks by ensuring that outputs do not reveal individual participation.

Question 2 – 金融消费行为中的差分隐私：一家金融研究机构分析交易数据以研究客户的消费行为。在发布研究洞见之前，为了保护机密性，机构对数据应用了差分隐私（Differential Privacy）。

Question 2 – Differential Privacy for Financial Spending Behavior: A financial research firm analyzes transaction data to study customer spending behavior. Before releasing insights, it applies Differential Privacy to preserve confidentiality.

A Question：差分隐私如何确保加入高消费用户（例如 C04）不会泄露其个人消费模式？

How does DP ensure that the inclusion of a high spender (C04) does not reveal individual spending patterns?

差分隐私通过添加噪声，使总体消费统计在加入或移除高消费用户时几乎不变。由于公布的数据变化极小，攻击者无法识别或推断 C04 的具体消费模式。

DP adds noise so that aggregate spending statistics remain nearly unchanged even when a high spender is included or removed. Because the published results barely shift, attackers cannot identify or infer C04’s specific spending pattern.

B Question：如果在“月收入”字段加入拉普拉斯噪声，这将如何影响收入与支出相关性的分析？

If Laplace noise is added to “Monthly Income,” how could this impact analysis of income–spending correlation?

加入拉普拉斯噪声会隐藏真实收入以保护隐私，但也可能削弱收入与支出之间的相关性。噪声可能扭曲趋势强度，使统计关系变弱或变得不够准确。

Adding Laplace noise hides exact income values, protecting privacy, but it can weaken the observed correlation between income and spending. The noise may distort trend strength or make statistical relationships appear weaker or less accurate.

C Question：解释选择不同的 ε 值如何影响基于差分隐私数据得出的经济洞察的可信度。Explain how choosing different ε values can influence the credibility of economic insights derived from DP-protected data.

较小的 ε 会加入更多噪声，虽然隐私更强但准确性降低，使经济分析结果可能不可靠。较大的 ε 能提供更准确的趋势，但隐私保护变弱。因此 ε 决定了可信分析与隐私保护之间的平衡。

A small ε adds strong noise, improving privacy but reducing accuracy, which can make economic insights unreliable. A large ε yields more accurate trends but weaker privacy protection. Thus, ε determines the balance between trustworthy analysis and confidentiality.

D Question：在存在辅助数据集的情况下，即使只共享平均值但不使用差分隐私，为什么仍然存在隐私泄露风险？

Why would sharing only averages without DP still risk privacy disclosure when auxiliary datasets exist?

没有差分隐私时，平均值可能被与外部数据集关联，从而推断个体信息，尤其是在小群体中。攻击者可以利用辅助数据（如年龄、类别、收入范围）来重建或缩小特定用户的消费范围。

Without DP, averages can be linked with external datasets to infer individual values, especially when groups are small. Attackers can combine auxiliary information (e.g., age, category, income range) to reconstruct or narrow down specific users’ spending.

Question 3 – 农业作物病害检测的联邦学习：五个智慧农业中心合作开发一个联邦学习（Federated Learning，FL）模型，利用无人机图像检测植物叶片病害。每个中心在本地训练模型，仅共享模型更新。

Question 3 – Federated Learning for Crop Disease Detection (Agriculture): Five smart agriculture centers collaborate to develop a Federated Learning (FL) model to detect plant leaf diseases using drone images. Each center trains locally and shares model updates only.

A Question：联邦学习如何在保护敏感农业数据的同时，实现协同学习？

How does FL protect sensitive agricultural data while still enabling collaborative learning?

联邦学习将所有无人机图像和原始农业数据保留在各自中心本地。系统只上传模型权重更新，而不上传原始图像，从而确保敏感农作物数据不会离开中心。

FL keeps all drone images and raw agricultural data stored locally at each center. Only model weight updates—not the original images—are sent to the server, ensuring sensitive field data never leaves the center.

B Question：如果某些中心的无人机传感器质量较差或疾病样本较少，会出现什么问题？What problem occurs if certain centers have poor-quality drone sensors or fewer disease samples?

低质量数据或样本数量不足会给全局模型带来噪声和偏差，从而降低模型在所有中心进行疾病检测时的整体性能。

Low-quality data or small sample sizes can weaken the global model by introducing noise and bias. This may cause the unified model to perform poorly when detecting diseases across all centers.

C Question：解释服务器如何将本地模型更新合并以创建统一模型。

Explain how local model updates are combined at the server to create a unified model.

服务器使用加权平均（如 FedAvg），根据各中心的数据量将其模型更新进行合并。形成的新全局模型随后会共享回所有中心。

The server performs weighted averaging (such as FedAvg), combining the model updates from each center based on their data size. This aggregated update forms the new global model shared back to all centers.

D Question：如果某个中心在训练过程中退出，联邦学习如何维持模型鲁棒性？

If one center drops out during training, how does FL maintain model robustness?

联邦学习可以继续使用其余中心的更新进行训练。由于不会依赖某一个中心的数据，系统具有韧性，即使参与节点减少，全局模型仍可继续收敛。

FL can continue training using updates from the remaining centers. Because no single center’s data is required, the system remains resilient and the global model can still converge with reduced but sufficient participation.

Why is FL useful for energy analytics across regions with strict data-sharing restrictions?

联邦学习允许各地区在不共享原始用户用电数据的情况下协同训练预测模型，从而在满足数据本地化和隐私要求的同时实现跨区域分析。

FL enables regions to collaboratively train energy prediction models without sharing raw consumer data, making it suitable for analytics under strict data-sharing and localization restrictions.

Regional differences in climate and temperature patterns can lead to varying energy usage behaviors, causing the global model to generalize poorly across all regions.

Question 5 – 医疗云分析中的同态加密：一家医院在将患者数据上传至云端分析服务用于 BMI 分析之前，先使用同态加密（Homomorphic Encryption，HE）对数据进行加密。Question 5 – Homomorphic Encryption for Healthcare Cloud Analytics: A hospital encrypts patient data using Homomorphic Encryption (HE) before uploading it to a cloud analytics service for BMI analysis.

A Question：同态加密（HE）如何让云服务器在看不到真实身高和体重的情况下计算 BMI（体重 / 身高²）？

How can HE allow the cloud server to compute BMI (Weight / Height²) without seeing actual height or weight?

同态加密允许在加密值上直接进行数学运算。服务器对加密后的身高和体重执行除法和平方运算，并生成加密的 BMI 结果，全程无需解密原始数据。HE supports mathematical operations directly on encrypted values. The server performs division and exponent operations on the encrypted height and weight, producing an encrypted BMI result without ever decrypting the inputs.

B Question：讨论同态加密在医疗分析工作负载中的计算挑战。

Discuss the computational challenges of HE in medical analytics workloads.

同态加密的计算成本非常高，需要大量算力和内存，导致延迟增加，使实时分析变得困难，并会显著降低大规模患者数据处理的速度。

HE operations are computationally expensive, requiring large processing power and memory. This increases latency, makes real-time analytics difficult, and can significantly slow down tasks such as large-scale patient data processing.

C Question：在将分析外包给第三方平台时，为什么同态加密比传统加密更安全？

Why is HE safer than traditional encryption when outsourcing analytics to third-party platforms?

传统加密需要在计算前解密数据，使第三方能看到明文信息。同态加密在处理过程中从不解密，云平台可以计算结果但永远无法看到敏感的患者数据。

With traditional encryption, data must be decrypted before computation, exposing it to the third party. HE never decrypts data during processing, so the cloud platform can compute results without ever seeing sensitive patient information.

D Question：同态加密如何符合《澳大利亚隐私原则》APP 11（个人信息安全）的要求？How does HE align with the Australian Privacy Principle (APP 11 – security of personal information)?同态加密确保个人信息在存储、传输和计算全过程保持加密状态。由于没有任何一方能接触明文数据，它有效满足 APP 11 对防止个人信息被滥用、干扰或未经授权访问的要求。HE keeps all personal information encrypted throughout storage, transfer, and computation. Because no party can access plaintext data, HE strongly supports APP 11’s requirement to protect personal information from misuse, interference, or unauthorized access.

Question 6 – 银行业务安全分析中的同态加密：一家银行使用同态加密（Homomorphic Encryption）在保护客户隐私的同时，对客户交易模式进行分析。Question 6 – Homomorphic Encryption for Secure Banking Analytics: A bank uses Homomorphic Encryption to analyze customer transaction patterns while maintaining privacy.

A Question：如何在看不到真实数值的情况下，用同态加密（HE）计算平均净余额（存款 − 贷款）？

How can HE be applied to calculate the average net balance (Deposit – Loan) without revealing actual values?银行利用同态加密在密文上直接进行“存款减贷款”和求平均的运算。服务器先对加密后的存款和贷款计算每位客户的加密净余额，再对这些净余额求加密平均值，最终结果只由授权方解密，因此在整个计算过程中不会暴露任何原始金额。

The bank performs subtraction and averaging directly on the encrypted deposit and loan values using HE. The server computes the encrypted net balance for each customer and then the encrypted average, which is only decrypted by the authorized party, so no raw amounts are exposed during processing.

B Question：同态加密在防范金融机构内部人员威胁方面有什么安全优势？

What security advantage does HE provide against insider threats in financial institutions?使用同态加密后，内部分析员或系统管理员看到的始终是加密数据，而非真实交易金额。由于分析过程中无需解密数据，内部人员几乎没有机会接触或滥用敏感的金融记录。

With HE, even internal analysts or administrators only see encrypted values, not real transaction amounts. Since data never needs to be decrypted on the analysis platform, insiders have far fewer opportunities to view or misuse sensitive financial records.

C Question：为什么在需要实时金融分析时，同态加密的计算延迟会成为一个问题？

Why is computation latency a concern in real-time financial analytics with HE?

同态加密在密文上的运算速度远慢于明文运算，复杂的加密计算会引入明显延迟。在实时银行业务场景（如欺诈检测或即时授信）中，这种延迟可能导致系统无法及时响应。

HE operations are much slower than operations on plaintext, so complex encrypted computations can introduce noticeable delays. In real-time banking scenarios (fraud detection, instant credit decisions), this latency may prevent the system from responding quickly enough.

D Question：从保护个人金融数据的角度比较同态加密和差分隐私。

Compare HE to Differential Privacy in terms of protecting individual financial data.

HE keeps each individual’s values fully encrypted during storage and computation, providing exact results without revealing data. Differential Privacy releases noisy aggregates: it hides any single person’s influence but does not protect the raw data once decrypted inside the system. HE protects data during processing, while DP protects privacy in the published outputs.
