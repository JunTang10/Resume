3. 对称加密 vs 非对称加密（Symmetric vs Public-Key Cryptography）

对称加密：发送者和接收者使用相同的密钥进行加解密。快速但密钥分发困难。

非对称加密（公钥密码）：每个用户都有一对公钥和私钥。公钥公开，私钥保密。解决了密钥分发问题，但计算较难。

English

Symmetric Encryption: The sender and receiver share the same key for encryption/decryption. Fast but key distribution is difficult.

Asymmetric (Public-Key) Encryption: Each user has a public and private key pair. The public key is known to all, the private key is kept secret. Solves the key distribution problem but is slower computationally.

🔍 什么是“网络战争”？

根据 Clausewitz 的战争定义，一个行为必须同时满足以下三项标准，才构成战争：暴力性（Violence）：必须存在实际或潜在的人身伤害或物理破坏

工具性（Instrumentality）：行为是有目的、为达成政治目标的手段

政治性（Politicality）：行为具有明确的政治动机和归属方Rid 的结论：目前没有任何网络攻击满足这三项标准，因此不能称之为“战争”。

According to Clausewitz, for an act to be “war” it must be:

Violent – must cause or threaten lethal force

Instrumental – must pursue a political end

Political – must be directed and attributed to a political actor

Rid argues: No cyber attack meets all three.

该定义是否过于狭窄？

Critics argue that Rid's reliance on Clausewitz’s classic war definition is too rigid and outdated in the digital era.

Attribution may be difficult, but attacks can still have political motives;

Modern conflict extends beyond physical violence to include digital infrastructure disruption;

Many ask whether our definition of war should evolve to reflect new realites.在网络空间中，政治归属是否真的可衡量？

Thomas Rid 的理论中，“政治归属”（political attribution） 是战争构成的三个必要条件之一。然而在现实中，网络攻击的归属极其困难，因为攻击者可以通过匿名代理、跳板服务器、加密通信等手段隐藏身份

In Rid’s framework, political attribution is a necessary element of war. However, in cyberspace, attribution is notoriously difficult due to anonymity, proxy servers, and technical obfuscation.

该理论是否已经过时？

Rid 的论文发表于 2012 年，当时网络攻击大多是间谍和破坏行为。但十多年后，网络空间已成为军事冲突中的核心战场

Rid’s theory, published in 2012, was appropriate for its time—when most cyber incidents were isolated acts of sabotage or espionage.

But in recent years, cyber operations have escalated in scope, intent, and coordination, as seen in:

Cyber operations in the Russia–Ukraine war

Ongoing cyber clashes between Iran and Israel

Strategic cyber campaigns between China and the US

Many now argue that Rid’s rigid framework no longer fits the evolving reality of hybrid warfare and integrated cyber strategies. The line between war and non-war has become increasingly blurred, challenging Rid’s conclusions.

- Misinformation: Inaccurate or misleading online content without intent to deceive.

- Disinformation: Similar content but with deliberate intent to mislead or harm

- Key difference: Intent.

- ✅ 1. Authentication & Cryptography✅ 1. 身份验证和加密

- Three-factor authentication (Repeated): Combines something you know (password), something you have (token), and something you are (biometrics) to provide the strongest level of identity verification. (Q21:1, Q2:5)三因素身份验证（重复） ：结合您已知的信息（密码）、您拥有的信息（令牌）以及您的身份信息（生物识别信息），提供最强级别的身份验证。（

- HMAC (Repeated): A hash-based message authentication code used to ensure both authentication and data integrity. (Q21:26, Q2:1)HMAC（重复） ：基于哈希的消息认证码，用于确保身份验证和数据完整性。

- Symmetric vs Asymmetric encryption (Repeated): Symmetric encryption (e.g., AES) is fast and suitable for large data; asymmetric encryption (e.g., RSA) is secure for key exchange.对称加密 vs 非对称加密（重复） ：对称加密（例如 AES）速度快，适用于大数据；非对称加密（例如 RSA）对于密钥交换而言是安全的。

- Diffie-Hellman vs RSA (Repeated): Diffie-Hellman offers perfect forward secrecy by generating new key pairs per session, unlike RSA. (Q21:28, Q2:14)Diffie-Hellman 与 RSA（重复） ：与 RSA 不同，Diffie-Hellman 通过每次会话生成新的密钥对，提供完美的前向保密性。(问题 21:28，问题 2:14)

- Digital signatures & non-repudiation: Signatures are created using the sender’s private key and verifiable by anyone with the public key, ensuring non-repudiation. (Q21:19)数字签名与不可否认性 ：签名由发送者的私钥创建，任何拥有公钥的人都可以验证，从而确保不可否认性。(问题 21:19)

- HMAC vs Digital Signature Efficiency (Repeated): HMAC is faster and better suited for bulk verification, while digital signatures offer stronger guarantees but are slower. (Q21:26, Q2:3)HMAC 与数字签名效率对比（重复） ：HMAC 速度更快，更适合批量验证；而数字签名虽然提供了更强的保障，但速度较慢。(问题 21:26，问题 2:3)

- bcrypt vs SHA-256: bcrypt is slower, includes salt, and resists brute force; SHA-256 is faster but lacks salting by default. (Q2:15)bcrypt 与 SHA-256 的比较 ：bcrypt 速度较慢，需要加盐，并且难以暴力破解；SHA-256 速度较快，但默认不加盐。(Q2:15)

- ✅ 2. Encryption Attacks & Defenses✅ 2. 加密攻击与防御

- Differential cryptanalysis (Repeated): Analyzes how input differences affect encryption outputs to exploit structural weaknesses. (Q21:2, Q2:6)差分密码分析（重复） ：分析输入差异如何影响加密输出，以利用结构性弱点。（Q21:2，Q2:6）

- Chosen ciphertext attack (CCA): Attackers use a decryption oracle to infer the key or plaintext. (Q21:20)选择密文攻击 (CCA) ：攻击者使用解密预言机推断密钥或明文。(问题 21:20)

- Buffer overflow (Repeated): Uses functions like gets() to overwrite return addresses and hijack control flow. (Q21:41; Q2:27,49)缓冲区溢出（重复） ：使用 gets() 等函数覆盖返回地址并劫持控制流。(

- Integer overflow: Operations like subtracting INT_MIN can result in logic errors and bypass conditions. 整数溢出 ：诸如减去 INT_MIN 之类的运算可能会导致逻辑错误和绕过条件。

- TOCTTOU (Repeated): Exploits the time gap between checking and using a resource. TOCTTOU：利用检查和使用资源之间的时间差。

- SQL injection (Repeated): Tricks like ' OR 1=1 bypass login validation; prepared statements can defend against this. (Q21:43,46; Q2:29,32)SQL 注入（重复） ：类似 ' OR 1=1 技巧可以绕过登录验证；准备好的语句可以防御这种攻击。（问题 21：43,46；问题 2：29,32）

- ✅ 3. Protocols & Network Security✅ 3. 协议和网络安全

- TLS security properties (Repeated): TLS uses symmetric encryption for confidentiality and MACs for integrity. (Q21:23, Q2:3)TLS 安全属性（重复） ：TLS 使用对称加密来确保机密性，并使用 MAC 来确保完整性。（问题 21:23，问题 2:3）

- TLS handshake and certificate validation (Repeated): Ensures the server’s identity to prevent MITM attacks. (Q21:15, Q2:3)TLS 握手和证书验证（重复） ：确保服务器身份，以防止中间人攻击。(Q21:15，Q2:3)

- IPsec: A protocol suite that encrypts and authenticates IP layer traffic. (Q2:4)IPsec ：用于加密和验证 IP 层流量的协议套件。(问题 2:4)

- Canary values (Repeated): Placed on the stack to detect buffer overflow before return address is overwritten. (Q21:50, Q2:36)检测值（重复） ：放置在堆栈上，用于在返回地址被覆盖之前检测缓冲区溢出。（Q21:50，Q2:36）

- Network layer responsibilities (Repeated): Handles logical IP addressing and routing. (Q21:49, Q2:35)网络层职责（重复） ：处理逻辑 IP 寻址和路由。（Q21:49，Q2:35）

- Tor & anonymity: Uses multi-hop onion routing to conceal both source and destination. (Q21:53)Tor 与匿名性 ：使用多跳洋葱路由来隐藏源和目标。(Q21:53)
